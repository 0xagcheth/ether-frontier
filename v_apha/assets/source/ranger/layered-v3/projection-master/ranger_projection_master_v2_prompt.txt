Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: revised assembled projection master for Ether Frontier Garrison Ranger tower

Create one compact fortified Garrison Ranger TOWER.

Structure:
- dense squat octagonal fortified tower footprint;
- raised central cardboard tower core seen directly from above;
- narrow hunter-green parapet ring with minimal empty deck;
- two small opposite recessed firing positions, each with one top-down hooded
  ranger token;
- compact fan-shaped rack of long green-fletched bolts attached to left-rear rim;
- small reserve bolt magazine attached to right rim;
- small rapid-volley crossbow on front sector of central core, occupying less
  than one fifth of the full silhouette;
- small green-amber hunting optic.

The central core must dominate. It must unmistakably read as an upgraded
defensive tower, not a broad platform, courtyard, arena, camp, bunker interior,
or giant game token.

Projection:
strict true top-down orthographic 90-degree camera. Only top surfaces and thin
cardboard cut edges. Compact centered composition, fully visible with padding.

Style:
handmade cartoon cardboard tabletop object; convincing corrugated tan cut edges,
matte grey stone-paper, warm brown wood, hunter-green fittings, restrained
crayon, thick uneven hand-painted ink outlines, child-cut imperfections, dirt,
glue marks, rubbed corners, and small local contact shadows between every glued
layer. Playful but materially believable.

Weapon logic:
mechanical rapid crossbow with physical string and long fletched bolts. No gun,
cannon, firearm barrel, shells, muzzle flame, gun smoke, explosion, energy beam,
or giant ballista.

Backdrop:
perfectly flat uniform solid #ff00ff chroma-key background with no shadow,
gradient, texture, floor, reflection, or vignette. Do not use #ff00ff inside the
tower.

Avoid:
broad platform, open arena, large empty deck, copied Watchtower body, dominant
giant crossbow, facade, doorway, staircase, side/rear view, isometric perspective,
terrain, labels, text, watermark, glossy 3D, or plastic.
