# World Lore

## Aeldrath

Aeldrath was shattered when the Demon Prince Varath tore open the Voidrift. Seven fortresses were raised around sealing crystals. Six have fallen. Grimhold is the last.

## Grimhold

Grimhold is an ancient fortress carved into a haunted mountain. It is cold stone, old gold seal-lines, broken walls, siege platforms, dead grass, and centuries of desperate repairs.

## The Warden

The player is the Warden: an immortal defender bound to the fortress by dying royal magic. The Warden cannot leave and cannot truly die while the seal remains.

## The Goblin Horde

The Horde is a scavenger civilization organized into loose clans:

- Wolf-pack and Warband: early runners, warriors, raiders, bruisers.
- Sappers and Tinkerers: tunnelers, bombers, machines, siege junk.
- Shaman Cult: witch-doctors, hex-wisps, summoners, bone magic.
- Beast-riders and Warlord Court: spiders, bat-riders, champions, bosses.

## Boss Canon

- Chapter 1: Goblin Warlord.
- Chapter 2: Grand Siege-Engine or Dread Goblin Warlord.
- Chapter 3: Captive Elder Wyrm.

## Naming

Names should feel old, martial, and carved into stone. Avoid jokey modern names for production-facing lore unless the line is intentionally spoken by a character.
