# Creative Direction

## Elevator Pitch

The last demon-seal is cracking. The Warden of Grimhold must defend an ancient mountain fortress from the Goblin Horde before the Voidrift consumes the world.

## Tone

Dark, mythic, dry, and tactical. The world is grim but not joyless. Humor should be sparse and character-based.

## Creative Pillars

- Last Stand: everything feels like the final line of defense.
- Cursed Legacy: Grimhold has centuries of scars, relics, and failed defenders.
- The Horde Corrupts And Scavenges: enemies are a unified invading warband using beasts, shamans, and junk siege craft.
- Tools Of Desperation: player towers are improvised fortress weapons, not polished machines.

## Enemy Canon

The enemy is one faction: the Goblin Horde.

Enemies should share:

- Green to grey-green skin, tusks, pointed ears, hooked noses, sinew, warts.
- Scrap iron, bone, leather straps, clan banners, crude paint, and scavenged siege material.
- Toxic-green ether for shaman magic, with sickly purple only as a secondary hex accent.
- Beasts and machines that are goblin-bred, goblin-built, piloted, chained, or branded.

Do not drift back to undead armies, abstract void-spawn, or unrelated monster factions unless the project vision is formally changed.

## Player Canon

The player represents Grimhold and the Warden. Player-side visuals use cold fortress stone, warm amber torchlight, gold seal magic, and desperate siege engineering.

## Narrative Delivery

- Briefings should feel like Warcraft III mission setup.
- Between-wave lines should be short.
- Lore unlocks can live in journals, fragments, inscriptions, and fortress relics.
- Boss death lines should be dramatic but concise.
