# Audio Style Guide

## Direction

Audio should sound old, heavy, and physical: wind through stone, strained wood, iron mechanisms, torch fire, distant war drums, and unstable magic.

## Ambience

- Mountain wind.
- Fortress creaks.
- Distant Horde movement.
- Low seal hum near the base.

## Music

Dark orchestral with percussive urgency. Boss waves may use choir stings and heavier drums. Avoid cheerful mobile-fantasy loops.

## Tower SFX

Towers should sound material-specific:

- Watchtower: bowstring, wood strain, arrow hit.
- Ballista/scorpion: heavy rope snap and bolt thunk.
- Cannon/mortar: compressed boom, stone echo, debris.
- Beacon: bright gold resonance.
- Obelisk: unstable captured magic crackle.

## Enemy SFX

Goblin voices should be sharp, rough, and varied by size. Machines clatter. Beasts snarl or screech. Shamans use wet, toxic, ritual sounds.

## Mix Rules

Gameplay-critical sounds win over ambience. UI feedback must be short and non-musical unless it confirms a major upgrade or wave result.
