# VFX Style Guide

## Purpose

VFX should clarify cause, target, damage type, and faction identity. Spectacle is secondary to readability.

## Player VFX

Player-side effects use warm amber, orange, gold, torchlight, and seal magic. They should feel like controlled fortress power.

## Horde VFX

Horde effects use toxic green with restrained sickly purple accents. They should feel alchemical, shamanic, scavenged, and unstable.

## Projectile Rules

- Projectiles must be readable at gameplay scale.
- Trails should be short.
- No projectile may obscure its target for more than a brief impact moment.
- Directional projectiles must match the owning unit projection.

## Impact Rules

Impacts should communicate damage type:

- Arrow/bolt: sharp contact, tiny splinters.
- Cannon/mortar: smoke, sparks, short shock.
- Beacon/seal: gold flash, clean pulse.
- Obelisk/Horde magic: colored rune crackle, not full-screen bloom.

## Forbidden

- Large soft bloom clouds.
- Modern particle soup.
- Effects that recolor the whole battlefield.
- Persistent glow that hides silhouettes.
