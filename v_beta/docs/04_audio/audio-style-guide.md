# Audio Style Guide

## Direction

Kitezh-17 sounds like an old research site operating beneath a living wooden
outpost: relay clicks, cable hum, wind in taiga, timber strain, stone contact,
paper handling and distant impossible signals.

## Ambient Layers

- wind over sopki and cedar;
- river or drainage water;
- radio hiss, relay clicks and transformer hum;
- timber creak, rope tension and stone settling;
- distant Lower City movement below the map;
- occasional archive tape, coded pulse or incomplete voice.

## Player Objects

- Дозорная весь: bowstring, dry wood and bolt impact;
- Плотницкая артель: saw, belt, logs and paper-fiber scrape;
- Каменная артель: pulley, stone strike and settling rubble;
- Камень Велеса: low wooden resonance and interrupted machine rhythm;
- Круг Дажьбога: warm metal ring and clear solar pulse;
- Катушка Перуна: copper buzz, relay snap and compact thunder.

## Enemies

- Ящеры: breath, scale, clay, bone and distinct caste voices;
- Серые: restrained filtered tones, lens focus and missing-frequency gaps;
- damaged Nav: reversed paper movement, incomplete whispers and hollow repeats;
- machines: faded motors, loose panels, cable strain and foreign ceramic clicks.

## Music

Use original composition. It may combine:

- restrained folk timbres without quoting a specific traditional melody;
- low choir or breath;
- late-Soviet electronic laboratory texture;
- percussion resembling wood, stone, metal and archive mechanisms.

No direct use of protected songs, military marches or sacred recordings.
