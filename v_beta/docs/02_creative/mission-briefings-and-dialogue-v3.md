# Ether Frontier — Mission Briefings and Dialogue v3

Status: **canonical for current wave milestones**  
Updated: 2026-07-24

## Voice rules

- short enough to read between waves;
- every claim belongs to a speaker;
- Rada distinguishes witness from evidence;
- the Grey Editor offers calm efficiency;
- Sarma never speaks for every serpent community;
- Mara appears only when an ending or repetition is at stake.

## Opening

**Rada:** “The Road will open when the broken bearing turns. I have a fragment
that says who comes through it. I do not yet have proof.”

**Zema:** “Build where the ground can carry an answer. A free cell is not an
empty cell.”

## After wave 1

**Rada:** “The fragment predicted runners. That makes it useful. It does not
make it ancient.”

## Wave 3 — first raids

**Zema:** “They are cutting the names from your worksites. Protect the labour
that makes the weapons possible.”

## Wave 5 — Red-Scale Voivode

**Sarma:** “His red plate is a military oath, not a species. Remove it and ask
which debt sent him upward.”

**Rada:** “I have two copies of that oath. Each calls the other counterfeit.”

## Hero unlock

**Rada:** “The towers have gathered enough Answer for one keeper. Choose a
method, not a ruler.”

## Wave 6 — air

**Amba:** “The sky Road does not touch your ground markers. Give it a separate
answer.”

## Wave 8 — Nav

**Mara:** “That which failed to finish is not the same as that which never
was. Do not call both of them empty.”

## Before wave 10

**Sarma:** “The second Road was below your map, not beyond it. The Prince will
open it whether he wins or dies.”

## After wave 10

**Grey Editor:** “Two Roads create disagreement. I can make them one.”

**Rada:** “That is not repair. It is deleting the fork.”

## Wave 11 — captured machines

**Zema:** “The chassis still remembers its maker. Break the pale directive,
not the craft beneath it.”

## Wave 15 — Sky Keeper

**Grey Editor:** “Three heads, three accounts, three unnecessary errors.”

**Amba:** “Three bearings. Remove one and the body falls from its own sky.”

## Wave 18 — simultaneous paths

**Rada:** “The Editor is testing whether we abandon one Road to make the other
simple.”

## Wave 20 — Empty-Name Engine

**Grey Editor:** “Your towers already share costs and rules. Let them share one
shape.”

**Binder:** “A shared rule is not a shared identity.”

## Wave 50 — final convergence

**Mara:** “You have defended this machine long enough to believe survival makes
it eternal. Choose what must end.”

**Sarma:** “The Serpent is awake. It is not waiting for your category.”

**Rada:** “Witnesses disagree. Evidence remains. That is enough to answer.”

## Failure lines

- **Rada:** “The Ledger lost a bearing. Record the loss before rebuilding.”
- **Zema:** “The ground remembers pressure even when the tower is gone.”
- **Grey Editor:** “One less name. The map is clearer.”
