## Naming rules

- runtime IDs never change during lore migration;
- Russian is the primary naming language;
- names describe function and circle, not secret-project terminology;
- no Kitezh-17, archive, laboratory or reality-editor vocabulary;
- no real ethnic group is used as an enemy category;
- names remain short enough for tower cards and wave previews.
