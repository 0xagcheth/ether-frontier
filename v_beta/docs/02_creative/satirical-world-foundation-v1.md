# Ether Frontier — Satirical World Foundation v1

Status: **new canonical world direction; detailed migration pending**  
Updated: 2026-07-26

The thematic subject sequence is owned by
`andrboll-topic-adaptation-map-v1.md`. This document defines the general
satirical world laws; it must not replace the concrete Andrboll-aligned topics
with a generic misinformation story.

## 1. Creative decision

Ether Frontier is not a fantasy confirmation of alternative history. It is a
satirical tower-defence world about how fragments of history, language, memory
and material culture are turned into one compulsory explanation.

The satire follows a research-documentary method:

1. show the original claim in its strongest and most attractive form;
2. preserve the small true fragment inside it;
3. expose the unsupported transition;
4. show how later speakers copy and enlarge that transition;
5. reveal the social, commercial or political use of the finished myth;
6. let absurdity emerge from escalation rather than mockery of believers.

No real ethnic or religious community is an enemy faction. The target is the
machinery of total explanation, source laundering and compulsory certainty.

## 2. Central premise

At the Last Knot, every Road brings testimony: a song, tool, burial, repair,
migration story, damaged book, family name or scientific measurement.
Testimonies may overlap without having one origin.

The world remains alive while each testimony keeps three detachable layers:

- **fragment** — what was actually found, said or remembered;
- **reading** — who interpreted it, when and for what purpose;
- **confidence** — how certain that reading may be.

The Grey Interval begins when those layers are fused. A reading loses its
author, uncertainty is removed, and the result is copied onto every fragment.

The Last Knot is not an archive of the one true past. It is a public machine
for keeping provenance visible.

## 3. Seven-stage satirical escalation

### I. Pleasant Resemblance

A word resembles another word; a motif resembles another motif. Comparison is
useful and harmless while marked as comparison.

Gameplay: duplicated route cards appear beside valid cards.

### II. Missing Bridge

Comparison becomes descent, borrowing becomes origin, and a gap is crossed
without evidence.

Gameplay: enemies place bridge cards between unrelated cells. Players separate
observation from inference.

### III. Ancient Original

A late text is presented as an ancient witness. Its missing original, unstable
language and publication history become proof of suppression.

Gameplay: copied tablets generate older-looking shells around new directives.
The shell is removed by restoring provenance, not by damage alone.

### IV. Complete Pantheon

Sparse and contradictory evidence becomes a detailed universal system. Empty
spaces are filled faster than surviving sources can limit them.

Gameplay: decorative modules attach to every structure and consume functional
sockets.

### V. Secret Arithmetic

Coincidence becomes code. Words, numbers, architecture and astronomy are
forced into one conversion table.

Gameplay: one Grey measure copies itself across unrelated Roads. Players make
incompatible measures coexist instead of finding a stronger universal formula.

### VI. Buried World

Every basement, reused stone and similar facade becomes evidence of one erased
global civilisation and one catastrophe.

Gameplay: false lower layers are inserted beneath real buildings, changing
their apparent age and ownership.

### VII. Enemy Required by the Theory

When a theory explains everything, disagreement needs editors, traitors,
destroyed moons or a global conspiracy.

Gameplay: the Grey Interval replaces people with categories. Here the satire
stops being playful and shows the harm of the system.

## 4. Player fantasy

The player is the **Binder**, not a chosen warrior or owner of true doctrine.
The Binder can:

- ask where a claim came from;
- keep original and interpretation physically separate;
- compare without declaring identity;
- return uncertainty to overconfident cards;
- restore an erased owner;
- let incompatible testimonies protect one Road together;
- revoke their own interpretation when better evidence arrives.

Victory is not proving the correct mythology. It is preventing any one
mythology, ideology or shortcut from becoming mandatory reality.

## 5. Seven Circles as instruments of inquiry

### Root — continuity without fabrication

Root asks whether a practice continued, was revived, or was newly created. It
can reconnect a tradition but leaves a visible graft scar.

### Stone — context and load

Stone remembers where an object was found, what lay around it, and how much
interpretation it can bear. A find removed from context becomes unstable.

### Thunder — public challenge

Thunder is the moment independent witnesses answer together. It interrupts a
claim only when different sources align; loudness gives no charge.

### Sun — exposure and comparison

Sun reveals edits, substitutions and modern layers. It does not declare truth;
it makes construction visible.

### Wind — transmission

Wind tracks how a phrase, image or story travelled. Repetition along one copied
route is not many independent witnesses.

### Water — borrowing and return

Water shows how motifs move between communities and change shape without
proving a single origin.

### Name — provenance and revocation

Name binds every claim to author, date, edition and confidence. A Name may be
disputed or returned. Anonymous certainty is weak.

## 6. Factions

### Road communities

Coalitions of practices, not proxies for whole peoples. Each contains
historians, performers, craftspeople, opportunists, believers and sceptics.

### Lower-Serpent communities

Holders of pressure records, underground routes and old obligations. Some
claims about them are true, some exaggerated, some imposed by surface readers.
They are never reptilian racial caricatures.

### Nav witnesses

Memories whose owners or endings are missing. They become dangerous when given
a convenient false name. Their conflict is tragic, not generic undead evil.

### Greys

Not aliens, an ethnicity or a cabal. They are an emergent editing process:
duplicated phrasing, erased uncertainty, identical correction plates and
citations that lead only to one another.

### Human antagonists

Each receives a useful fragment, persuasive motive and a point where they
choose certainty over revocability. Satire targets that choice, not stupidity.

## 7. Dialogue rule

Comedy comes from confident escalation and material consequences.

> “These cards have the same notch.”  
> “So they may have shared a cutter.”  
> “Or one copied the other.”  
> “Exactly. Therefore the cutter built the moon.”

Avoid silly names as the main joke, extremist slogans as flavour, mock-Slavic
gibberish, pseudo-runes, sacred decoration and an infallible narrator.

## 8. Mission evidence structure

Each mission contains four separable layers:

1. **claim card** — what an in-world speaker asserts;
2. **primary trace** — text, object, recording or remembered practice;
3. **comparison card** — what else may explain the trace;
4. **confidence tab** — known, plausible, disputed, contradicted or unknown.

Waves attack these relationships. A successful defence may end with unresolved
cards. **Unknown is a valid protected state.**

## 9. Use of real-world material

Real names, books and movements belong in research notes, not direct factions.
The game transforms recurring mechanisms:

- folk etymology;
- late text presented as ancient;
- visual pareidolia;
- genetic category treated as language or nation;
- absence of evidence reframed as suppression;
- architectural resemblance expanded into global history;
- criticism reframed as participation in conspiracy.

Every episode combines several sources and fictionalises names, places and
objects. The game critiques a method and does not reproduce Andrboll's text,
jokes or documentary structure.

## 10. Migration consequences

This direction supersedes older framing wherever it:

- treats speculative cosmology as objective world history;
- uses Greys as literal alien or racial beings;
- turns contested terms into universal metaphysical facts;
- presents Seven Roads as proof of one ancient hidden system;
- gives the Binder or narrator final epistemic authority.

The Last Knot, Roads, Circles, Mother Earth, Nav and material grammar remain,
but must be rewritten through provenance, confidence and revocation.
