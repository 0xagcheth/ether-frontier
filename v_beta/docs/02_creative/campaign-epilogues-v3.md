# Ether Frontier — Campaign Epilogues v3

Status: **canonical narrative outcomes; implementation pending**  
Updated: 2026-07-24

## Ending I — The restored order

The Binder restores one authoritative Ledger and seals the Grey Interval.
Roads open on time and names return reliably. Rada records contradiction in the
margins, but only the central reading operates the machine. Peace returns with
the risk that stability may again be mistaken for truth.

Final image: one strong central cardboard ring, seven coloured Roads fitted
cleanly into it, small handwritten doubts underneath.

## Ending II — The many-road covenant

The Ledger is divided among witnesses. No keeper can open every Road. A route
exists only when several records agree on an action without agreeing on one
origin. The world becomes slower to command and harder for the Grey Editor to
capture.

Final image: seven separate punched ledgers around an open centre, joined by
temporary fibre bridges.

## Ending III — Mara's closure

The Binder accepts that the old engine has completed its duty. Mara unbinds it
while defenders hold the last pulse. Communities retain local crossings and
must build a new Knot later. The Greys lose their single target.

Final image: the central machine separated into named cardboard components;
roots and water return while seven small fires remain.

## Shared conclusion

No ending validates racial purity, one ancestral civilisation or a universal
secret language. The choice concerns governance of memory:

- one stable reading;
- distributed agreement;
- honest completion and renewal.
