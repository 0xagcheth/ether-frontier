# Ether Frontier — Faction Bible v5

Status: **canonical**  
Updated: 2026-07-24

## Keepers of the Last Knot

Goal: keep several Roads connected without forcing one origin.

Material grammar:

- warm, individually repaired punchboard;
- owner-specific colour and attachment logic;
- visible provenance tabs;
- asymmetry caused by repair rather than corruption.

Internal conflict: central coordination versus local autonomy.

## Lower-Serpent communities

Goal: protect depth water, pressure routes, homes and conflicting treaties.

They are plural:

- Red-Scale military retainers;
- pressure diggers and workers;
- winged scouts;
- independent treaty houses;
- Sarma's cartographic delegation.

Material grammar:

- warm clay paper;
- shed-scale cards;
- pressure-fit stone;
- heat marks and underground water traces.

They are never a biological evil or proxy for a real group.

## Nav witnesses

Goal: complete an interrupted obligation or acquire a name that fits.

Material grammar:

- translucent torn paper;
- incomplete contour;
- loose threads and movable name scraps;
- soft missing centres.

Some can be restored. Destruction is not their only resolution.

## Greys

Goal: eliminate ambiguity by reducing every relation to a reusable directive.

Material grammar:

- pale correction overlays;
- repeated identical parts;
- erased or replaced labels;
- too-perfect symmetry;
- cold gaps where local children were removed.

The Grey Editor is a distributed voice, not a biological sovereign.

## Captured craft

Original faction remains visible underneath.

Material grammar:

- coherent colourful chassis;
- removable Grey directive plate;
- missing owner cloth, ammunition or name child;
- copied replacement parts that almost fit.

## Mara's Keepers

Independent agents of lawful ending. They do not join waves as a species.

Material grammar:

- dark berry, winter flax, compost brown and clean negative space;
- closure threads and removable last-door cards;
- no skull cult or generic necromancer styling.
