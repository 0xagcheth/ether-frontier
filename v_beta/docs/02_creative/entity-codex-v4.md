## Global production constraints

- one family per atlas;
- true top-down for layered gameplay objects;
- stable footprint during normal animation;
- every moving or glowing element is a child layer;
- projectile, impact and unique debris belong to the owner atlas;
- no real ethnic group maps to an enemy ID;
- no Kitezh-17, archive laboratory or reality-editor terminology;
- runtime IDs remain unchanged.
