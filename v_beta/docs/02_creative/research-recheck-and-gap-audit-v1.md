# Ether Frontier — Research Recheck and Gap Audit v1

Status: **blocking audit before Lore Bible v6**  
Updated: 2026-07-24

## 1. Audit result

The current research direction is usable, but it is **not yet complete enough
to become the final lore canon**.

The first pass correctly separated alternative history from documented
tradition and correctly prohibited ethnic coding of enemies. It nevertheless
made several creative syntheses sound more inherited or comprehensive than
they are. This audit corrects that impression.

## 2. What was rechecked

- the user-provided audit of Andrboll's working `Веды` script;
- the current research, source matrix and cultural integration brief;
- academic/reference material on named Slavic deities and lower mythology;
- scholarly criticism of the Book of Veles;
- documented Mari cosmogonic motifs;
- Bashkir epic and performance context;
- UNESCO descriptions of Kyrgyz `Manas`, Uzbek/Karakalpak bakhshi art and
  Sakha/Yakut Olonkho;
- the internal connection between cosmology, tower-defence rules and modular
  object art.

## 3. Confirmed findings

### Andrboll is a metanarrative source

The working script is a satirical investigation of how attractive speculation
can escalate into a closed historical system. Its essential movement is:

1. playful word association and cultural longing;
2. the promise of a lost authentic text;
3. a total ancestral cosmology;
4. pseudo-scientific confirmation;
5. conspiratorial immunity to disproof;
6. racial hierarchy and political radicalisation.

The game currently takes stages 1–4 as imagery but does not yet dramatise this
escalation strongly enough. That is a major narrative gap.

### The Book of Veles must remain disputed

The surviving publication history, missing originals and linguistic analysis
do not justify using the Book as an authentic ancient source. It is appropriate
as an in-world disputed artefact and as a study of reception, belief and
identity formation.

### Named Slavic figures are not a complete recovered system

Medieval evidence supports several names and functions, including Perun,
Volos/Veles, Mokosh, Dazhbog and Stribog. It does not supply the detailed,
uniform, pan-Slavic theology often shown in modern diagrams.

### Mother Moist Earth is a supported folkloric image

The phrase and maternal/generative treatment of earth occur in recorded
folklore. The game's planetary health, build-cell memory and anti-extraction
system are original design extrapolations, not inherited doctrine.

### Mara is regionally complex

Related terms and figures are documented around death, nightmare, disease and
seasonal practices in different Slavic regions. `Mara, Keeper of the Last
Door` and the ethical concept of lawful ending are original game fiction. They
must be labelled and written as such.

### The selected epic traditions are real but mechanically transformed

Manas, bakhshi art and Olonkho are documented living performance traditions.
Their use in ordered abilities, route restoration or layer switching is a game
transformation. No exact verse, ritual, garment or sacred object should be
copied without a narrower review.

## 4. Corrections to the first pass

### Correction A — Seven Roads are not source-derived

`Seven Roads` is an Ether Frontier organising device. Seven is not a verified
shared sacred number connecting all selected cultures. The structure may stay
because it fits Option C and tower-defence readability, but the fiction must
show that the Binder constructed the coalition; it was not revealed as the
secret original map of humanity.

### Correction B — Yav/Nav/Prav cannot be the objective universal cosmos

The complete triad is heavily mediated by the disputed Book of Veles and modern
neo-pagan reception. It may be the vocabulary of one in-world school. Other
Roads must retain different and sometimes incompatible cosmologies.

### Correction C — Daariya is a loaded name

`Daariya` is closely associated with the Slavic-Aryan alternative-history
corpus and its racial cosmology. Even when the game rejects that ideology, the
name imports it.

Decision required before canon:

- retain the name only as the self-name used in a compromised in-world text;
- give the actual ruins a neutral fictional archaeological designation; or
- replace the name entirely.

The current brief must not silently normalise Daariya as a historical culture.

### Correction D — Mara's current role is authored fiction

The role is conceptually strong and should probably remain, but the lore bible
must say that Ether Frontier combines several associations into a new
character. It is not a reconstruction of a universally worshipped ancient
goddess.

### Correction E — cultural coverage is incomplete

The phrase “peoples of the Soviet and post-Soviet space” cannot yet be claimed.
The present lanes are selective research directions, not comprehensive
coverage.

Material still under-researched includes:

- Belarusian and Ukrainian regional variation rather than a single broad
  East-Slavic bucket;
- Tatar traditions beyond being separated from Bashkir material;
- Chuvash, Udmurt, Komi, Erzya and Moksha traditions individually;
- Kazakh, Tajik, Turkmen and Karakalpak differences beyond epic-performance
  shorthand;
- Buryat, Kalmyk, Tuvan, Altai, Khakas and Mongolic traditions;
- Evenki, Even, Nenets, Nganasan, Chukchi, Koryak, Itelmen, Nanai, Udege, Nivkh
  and other northern/Far-Eastern peoples individually;
- Moldovan, Gagauz and Roma traditions;
- Estonian and Latvian material separately from Lithuanian material;
- Georgian, Armenian, Azerbaijani and each North-Caucasian tradition beyond a
  broad holding category.

Trying to fill all of these at equal depth would produce tokenism. The canon
should select only the lanes required by the actual campaign, document them
deeply and leave the rest unclaimed.

### Correction F — the Soviet/post-Soviet layer is too generic

Constructivism, mosaics, enamel signs and repair marks are only a beginning.
The current text does not yet distinguish:

- 1920s avant-garde print;
- mid-century scientific and educational illustration;
- regional modernism and public art;
- late-Soviet consumer printing and tabletop games;
- post-Soviet reuse, market materials and local repair practices.

A visual-source board with dated, attributed examples is required before this
layer enters concept-art prompts.

### Correction G — character names need review

`Veda` risks turning a textual/religious term into a generic woman's name and
may make the Andrboll reference too literal. `Mera` is visually and phonetically
too close to `Mara`. Both remain working names only.

### Correction H — object coverage has not been performed

The integration brief explains principles but does not yet map them to every
existing runtime entity. It therefore does not satisfy the earlier requirement
to describe all characters, enemies, towers, buildings, projectiles, effects
and resources.

No sprite prompt should be considered lore-correct until an entity-by-entity
migration table exists.

## 5. Internal logic audit

### What works

- Greys erase relations rather than represent a race;
- Mara's ending and Grey deletion are meaningfully different;
- roads cannot simply be closed because allies, water, seasons and memory also
  need passage;
- towers as assembled witnesses supports modular layered sprites;
- captured towers becoming visually generic supports both lore and gameplay;
- ground strain connects Mother Earth to tower placement rather than leaving
  her as exposition.

### What still needs an answer

1. Why do Greys attack in discrete waves rather than continuously?
2. What is the exact relationship between the Grey Editor and individual Grey
   bodies?
3. Why can physical weapons damage entities defined by erasure?
4. What happens to a named being killed normally versus erased by a Grey?
5. What prevents the defenders from copying infinite witness modules?
6. Who built the Bearing Ledger, and why can no one relocate it?
7. Is the player defending one battlefield, several regional Knots or a moving
   campaign route?
8. How does a tower's cultural provenance affect mechanics without assigning a
   real culture to a weapon stereotype?
9. Which existing tower is the Watchtower in the new fiction, and why does it
   use a crossbow, if it still does?
10. Which game ending resolves the temptation of a single perfect origin?

These questions must be answered in Lore Bible v6 before campaign dialogue and
asset prompts are rewritten.

## 6. Safety and representation audit

Confirmed rules:

- no real people become Greys, serpent people, corrupted bloodlines or enemy
  races;
- discriminatory claims in the Andrboll source are shown only as objects of
  criticism, manipulation or escalation;
- no living sacred sign becomes a generic tower badge;
- cultural inspiration is credited internally at the level of a specific
  tradition, process and source;
- shared Soviet history does not erase older, local, Islamic, Christian,
  Jewish, Buddhist or living indigenous contexts;
- “political correctness” is not handled by euphemism: harmful claims are
  excluded from world truth and their persuasive mechanism is examined.

## 8. Final assessment

The research has a viable core, but the correct status is:

> **promising and structured, not exhaustive, not yet canon-ready.**

The greatest strength is the conflict between living plural memory and Grey
uniformity. The greatest current risk is reproducing the very totalising move
that Andrboll criticises: assembling many traditions into one elegant invented
system and then forgetting that the system was invented.
