# Ether Frontier — Lore Source Matrix v1

Status: **research and adaptation control; not yet game canon**  
Updated: 2026-07-24

## 1. Purpose

This matrix prevents three different things from being silently blended:

1. documented mythology, folklore, craft or performance;
2. scholarly reconstruction and modern revival;
3. alternative history, satire and original game fiction.

The game may use all three, but it must tell the team which one it is using.
`Reliability` describes the historical claim, not the artistic value.

### Reliability scale

- **A — documented tradition:** historical sources, academic reference works,
  community-backed UNESCO materials or documented living practice;
- **B — cautious reconstruction:** a scholarly interpretation assembled from
  incomplete evidence and regional parallels;
- **C — modern synthesis:** neo-pagan, literary or popular system that may be
  culturally influential but is not evidence of an intact ancient doctrine;
- **D — alternative history:** unsupported historical claim;
- **F — original fiction:** created for Ether Frontier.

## 3. Source handling rules

### For writers

Every lore assertion must be tagged internally as:

- `witness`: who says it;
- `evidence`: what can be checked in the game world;
- `interpretation`: what model connects the evidence;
- `effect`: what the belief enables in play.

A narrator may be wrong. The codex may preserve two incompatible accounts.
Mechanical usefulness does not prove historical truth.

### For artists

Every culturally influenced prompt must specify:

- lane and exact community/tradition;
- source principle;
- material or construction process being transformed;
- distance from the source;
- prohibited direct copies;
- whether the result is diegetic, UI-only or review-only.

Prefer structure and process over copied iconography. “Inspired by oral
counterpoint” is safer and more useful than “put a sacred sign on the tower”.

### For enemy design

Enemy categories describe a metaphysical action:

- erases names;
- repeats a body;
- captures a machine;
- falsifies a route;
- interrupts a response;
- extracts without returning;
- forces incompatible memories into one authorised story.

They never describe skin colour, religion, language, ethnicity, nationality or
disability.

## 4. Primary research anchors

- Andrboll working script `Веды`, supplied by the user;
- user-supplied source audit and transcript notes;
- Big Russian Encyclopedia: Slavs, Veles, Slavic demonology, Mari, Yumo,
  Olonkho, traditional cultures and literatures of the peoples of Russia;
- UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage: Bakhshi art, Manas, Olonkho,
  Ural-Batyr/Akbuzat materials, Alpamys, Daredevils of Sassoun and sutartinės;
- Russian Museum collection materials for Soviet print and constructivist
  composition.

This is a design-provenance matrix, not a substitute for consultation with
living tradition bearers when a production asset approaches a sacred object,
ritual practice or recognisable community emblem.
