## 3. World-logic check

PASS:

- The Mother Earth is the living substrate, not a universal humanlike queen.
- Nav is unfinished memory; Mara is lawful ending; Grey erasure is forced
  sameness. These three functions no longer collapse into generic death magic.
- Lower-Serpent communities are inhabited political societies, not Nav, hell or
  a racial enemy.
- Greys are a distributed editing protocol, not an ethnicity.
- The Bearing Ledger explains why the Last Knot matters materially.
- The Last Knot cannot be moved because its bearings include local watershed,
  roots, graves, underground pressure and promises.
- Seven Roads are cultural-practice coalitions.
- Seven Circles are functional craft assemblies: Root, Stone, Thunder, Sun,
  Wind, Water and Name.
- A Circle needs witness, material, use and accepted risk from Roads; therefore
  no culture is assigned an essential military or mystical function.

## 4. Conflict and plot check

PASS:

- Serpent attacks arise from conflicting treaties, military factions and Grey
  pressure rather than inherent evil.
- Nav attacks arise from interrupted obligations and false missing names.
- Captured machines preserve the original craft under detachable directives.
- The Grey Editor's escalation is coherent: useful fragment → total explanation
  → immunity to contradiction → purity demand → erasure.
- Mara creates a final conflict with endless defence by requiring obsolete
  forms to end honestly.
- All three endings answer the same governance question: central stable
  reading, distributed covenant or closure and renewal.
