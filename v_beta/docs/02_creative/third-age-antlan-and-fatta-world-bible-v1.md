# Ether Frontier — Third Age: Antlan and Fatta World Bible v1

Status: **canonical third-age world layer**  
Updated: 2026-07-26

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the third age: the southern civilisations founded after
Daariya, the rise of the Ants, the island federation of Antlan, the political
use of Fatta, the War of Corrected Heaven and the catastrophe that destroys the
moon and drowns Antlan.

The age adapts the Antlan/Atlantis, nuclear-war and second-lunar-catastrophe
cycle of the pseudo-history examined by Andrboll. It is fictional canon, not a
claim about archaeology, ancient peoples or real flood traditions.

Its central question is: what happens when a civilisation that survived one
edited catastrophe builds its entire state around never being surprised again?

## 2. The world after Daariya

The survivors settle across Rassenia, the Riphean corridor, warm inland seas
and western islands. They do not found one continuous empire. They establish
hundreds of covenants that share fragments of Daariyan practice.

Three changes define the new age:

1. temporary evacuation offices become permanent governments;
2. fleet specialisations become hereditary estates;
3. possession of a surviving archive becomes a claim to rule.

The four lineages remain real civic traditions, but registries increasingly
assign them at birth. Mixed settlements are common; purity law exists mainly
on paper until rulers discover that ancestry certificates are taxable.

## 3. Deya, Lutetia and Arey

Refugees arriving through damaged passages bring mutually incompatible
accounts of an older cosmic disaster.

### What can be established

- Deya was a mobile inhabited world linked to the Golden Path;
- Lutetia was its passage-regulating satellite;
- a strategic intervention broke Lutetia and damaged nearby routes;
- Arey's atmosphere and surface infrastructure were devastated;
- many Arey survivors moved underground or were transferred to Midgard;
- Fatta reached Midgard through the same emergency operation.

### What cannot be established

No record proves whether Deya was destroyed, hidden in Interworld or saved.
Every government prefers the version that legitimises its current border.

The stones between planetary routes are later called the remains of one
exploded world. Material analysis shows fragments from several bodies. The
Ministry of Singular Causes labels this confirmation.

## 4. Fatta, the adopted moon

Fatta is not a natural moon of Midgard. It is a vast damaged reservoir and
passage counterweight transferred from the Deya system before the First
Landing.

Its thirteen-day circuit stabilises several interworld routes but produces
severe tidal and gravitational stress. Daariya maintained it through a
distributed lunar covenant. After the flood, control fragments among rival
states.

Fatta becomes four things at once:

- a shared piece of dangerous infrastructure;
- a memorial to displaced Deya and Arey peoples;
- a strategic weapon no state can allow another to control;
- proof, according to priests, that the number thirteen is cosmically fatal.

The wordplay between Fatta, fate and fatality is a later sermon. Within the
world it is memorable, popular and linguistically false.

## 5. The Ants

The **Ants** begin as mixed mobile communities maintaining long-distance
bridges between survivor settlements. Their name derives from an old route
term meaning a load carried across an interval. Later genealogists invent a
single heroic ancestor named Ant.

They become known for:

- hydraulic engineering;
- distributed civic councils;
- tidal calculation;
- copper memory channels;
- accepting refugees without assigning them a single lineage;
- keeping important records in both machines and living witnesses.

The last practice initially protects them from edited archives. Their eventual
attempt to eliminate the human witness becomes Antlan's fatal mistake.

## 6. Founding Antlan

Antlan is built across a western island chain above a junction of oceanic and
subterranean pressure routes. It is not one concentric golden capital. It is a
federation of inhabited rings connected by tide locks, causeways and floating
archive courts.

The name means the Ants' maintained crossing. Later expeditions hear a similar
foreign name for a drowned island and declare every account proof of the same
lost empire.

### The seven civic rings

1. **Witness Ring** — public testimony and revision.
2. **Copper Ring** — hydraulic memory channels.
3. **Tide Ring** — water and climate regulation.
4. **Guest Ring** — refugees from Arey, Deya and Lower Passages.
5. **Measure Ring** — Fatta observation.
6. **Forge Ring** — strategic infrastructure.
7. **Crown Ring** — emergency coordination, initially temporary.

The Crown Ring's first legal text says it will dissolve when the emergency is
over. Every later edition defines the current emergency in the present tense.

## 7. The Antlan achievement

Antlan turns dangerous inherited systems toward public use:

- Fatta's pull drives tide engines;
- pressure channels distribute fresh water;
- climate mirrors protect crops;
- controlled ground resonators prevent earthquakes;
- continuity vessels preserve the dying long enough for final testimony;
- memory channels allow any citizen to inspect revisions to law.

It becomes wealthy because it makes catastrophe-management ordinary.

Its weakness follows from the same success: every crisis becomes evidence that
more of the world should be placed under Antlan's management.

## 8. The doctrine of the Last Surprise

The descendants of Daariya remember that their leaders hid Spasova's forecast.
Antlan's response is the **Doctrine of the Last Surprise**:

> Nothing capable of changing the world may remain outside the world model.

At first this means transparent measurement. Gradually it means compulsory
measurement, then predictive policing, then destruction of anything the model
cannot classify.

Living witnesses become a problem because they remember context differently.
The Crown Ring proposes an independent perfect archive that will no longer need
fallible people.

## 9. Net-Miru

**Net-Miru** is a massive wandering body whose periodic approach disturbs the
Golden Path. The name originally means “not belonging to the measured peace”.
It is not a hidden planet populated by a single evil race.

Antlan astronomers predict that its next passage will destabilise Fatta.
Models disagree about the date, distance and consequence.

The Crown Ring chooses the most catastrophic model because it grants the
widest emergency authority. Its opponents choose the mildest because it
protects trade. Both accuse the other of falsifying the sky.

## 10. The League of Empty Measure

The Crown Ring, strategic engineers and archive priests form the **League of
Empty Measure**. They intend to correct Net-Miru's path by using Fatta as a
gravitational lever.

Their system requires:

- removal of Fatta's ancient safety limits;
- synchronisation of Antlan's tide and tectonic engines;
- strategic charges beneath rival continental stations;
- exclusive control of the perfect archive;
- classification of dissenting witnesses as corrupted data.

The League is not secretly controlled by Greys. Some copied persons work for
it, some oppose it, and most members are ordinary Antlan citizens convinced
that only total control can prevent another Lelya.

## 11. Priests of the Unbroken Chronicle

The priests preserve the heroic version of Daariya's fall. They teach that
Dazhbog's victory was perfect and the flood came only from enemy wickedness.

This creates a political trap:

- admitting uncertainty about Fatta would question the old heroic history;
- acknowledging mixed responsibility would weaken lineage authority;
- recognising lunar civilians would challenge the concept of an enemy race;
- publishing Spasova's forecast would prove that sacred history was edited.

The priests therefore bless the League's intervention as a repetition of
Dazhbog's victory, despite Dazhbog's omitted ninth book forbidding precisely
such unaccountable action.

## 12. The opposition

### Living Witness Assembly

Archivists, actors, elders and copied persons who insist that a record without
a revisable witness becomes a command rather than memory.

### Tide Commons

Workers maintaining water systems. They understand that the gravitational plan
will drown the outer rings even if it succeeds.

### Arey Underground Compact

Refugees whose world was already destroyed by strategic sky engineering. Their
warnings are dismissed as ancestral trauma rather than technical experience.

### Lower Water Covenant

Naga and Deep Scale communities maintaining the pressure routes beneath the
islands. They demand the right to shut systems whose surface owners overload
them.

### Fatta Keepers

Descendants and constructed continuities of Deya's refugees. They regard Fatta
as a living memorial, not Antlan property.

## 13. The Greys and the invented foreign hand

The League needs the Antlan conflict to appear external. Its publicity office
publishes a diagram connecting:

- Peklo signals;
- copied bodies;
- Grey refugees;
- serpent tunnels;
- foreign merchants;
- opponents of the Crown Ring.

The lines on the diagram indicate contact, suspicion, shared address, similar
ink or no recorded relationship. Later copies print every line identically.

There are genuine agents exploiting the crisis, including a Peklo mimetic cell
trying to open the unstable passage. They do not cause Antlan's political
decision. The state expands their limited operation into a total explanation
that absolves its own institutions.

## 14. Weapons of the War of Corrected Heaven

The civilisation does not possess generic magical superweapons. It weaponises
systems built for survival.

### Gravitational engines

Fatta-balancing anchors are retuned to alter the path of Net-Miru. Their recoil
changes local tides and stresses the moon.

### Tectonic resonators

Devices built to release ground pressure gradually are synchronised beneath
enemy cities. A defensive maintenance network becomes an earthquake weapon.

### Climate mirrors

Agricultural reflectors redirect heat, cloud and prevailing wind. Their use
creates crop failure far beyond intended targets.

### Nuclear foundry charges

Compact star-fire devices normally open or seal deep construction chambers.
Both sides deploy them against resonators and archives. Their glass fields
later become proof of whichever ancient global nuclear war a lecturer already
believes in.

### Memory weapons

The perfect archive can overwrite civic cards and copied bodies with one
authorised version of an event. It cannot change the past, but it changes what
infrastructure recognises as having happened.

## 15. The War of Corrected Heaven

The conflict begins as a constitutional dispute and expands through seven
escalations:

1. the Crown Ring extends emergency authority;
2. the Tide Commons refuses Fatta synchronisation;
3. the League replaces workers with directive copies;
4. continental states attack Antlan's resonators;
5. Antlan answers with climate and tectonic strikes;
6. nuclear foundry charges break pressure routes;
7. rival commands reach Fatta simultaneously.

Because all sides inherit related Daariyan systems, the war is visibly a civil
war. Later chronicles recolour one army as servants of Dark worlds so that the
myth of an eternally unified Great Race can survive.

## 16. The breaking of Fatta

The League orders Fatta to pull against Net-Miru. The Fatta Keepers order it to
disconnect from Midgard. Continental forces order its anchors to hold position.

All three commands are individually survivable. Their simultaneous execution
is not.

Fatta's layered systems separate. Unlike Lelya, it does not fall as one field
of fragments. Its mass enters the oceanic pressure network in a chain of
impacts while a large remnant is lost through Interworld.

Whether the intervention changes Net-Miru's course remains unknown. The body
passes without the predicted global collision, allowing every surviving
faction to claim its own action saved the world.

## 17. The drowning of Antlan

Antlan falls through the interaction of systems its people built:

- Fatta fragments strike the western ocean;
- tide engines amplify rather than absorb the displacement;
- sabotaged pressure channels split the island foundation;
- tectonic resonators release together;
- nuclear chambers vitrify drainage routes;
- the perfect archive locks gates according to an obsolete map;
- the Crown Ring remains above water longest because it diverted protection
  from the outer rings.

Antlan does not vanish in one wave. The seven rings fail over several days,
leaving time for rescue, betrayal and deliberate destruction of evidence.

## 18. The last archive

As water reaches the Witness Ring, the perfect archive offers to preserve one
final unified account. Accepting would save enormous technical knowledge but
erase contradictory witnesses from every machine linked to it.

The Living Witness Assembly breaks the central index. Records survive as
waterlogged fragments scattered across different cultures.

This is why Antlan is remembered everywhere and proven nowhere: not because
every myth describes one real historical Atlantis, but because displaced
Antlan communities carry different pieces of a deliberately fragmented record
inside the fictional world.

## 19. What later histories claim

### Antlan imperial version

Foreign Grey agents corrupted rebellious workers and forced the righteous
Crown to use forbidden weapons.

### Continental victory version

Pure northern descendants defeated a decadent island race before it could
enslave the world.

### Priest version

The gods drowned Antlan for abandoning ancestral law.

### Technocratic version

One calculation error destroyed an otherwise perfect system.

### Surviving evidence

The catastrophe required ideology, secrecy, institutional incentives, damaged
infrastructure and weapons used by several sides. No single villain explains
it.

## 20. Third-age factions

- **Antlan Seven-Ring Federation** — innovative civic federation becoming an
  emergency empire.
- **League of Empty Measure** — attempts to remove uncertainty from governance.
- **Living Witness Assembly** — protects revisable memory.
- **Tide Commons** — infrastructure workers resisting strategic misuse.
- **Fatta Keepers** — displaced custodians of the adopted moon.
- **Arey Underground Compact** — survivors who recognise the repeating pattern.
- **Continental Covenant States** — rivals frightened by Antlan control and
  willing to use the same weapons.
- **Unbroken Chronicle** — priests who convert edited disaster memory into
  political authority.

## 21. Gameable dramatic arc

### Movement 1 — City that remembers

Build Antlan's civic rings, share water and inspect public revisions. The city
must feel genuinely admirable.

### Movement 2 — The thirteenth moon-path

Restore Fatta's damaged systems with Deya refugees and discover that nobody
owns a complete operating covenant.

### Movement 3 — Last Surprise

Investigate Net-Miru forecasts. Each model is useful, incomplete and politically
sponsored.

### Movement 4 — Foreign hand

Fight a real mimetic cell while preventing the registry from turning all Grey,
serpent and foreign residents into one enemy class.

### Movement 5 — Civil war

Defend infrastructure whose offensive and protective functions are the same.
Every activated strategic system worsens a later disaster state.

### Movement 6 — Correct the heaven

Board or signal Fatta to separate competing commands. Full success is no longer
possible; the player determines the fragment paths and survival zones.

### Movement 7 — Drowned witness

Evacuate Antlan while deciding whether to preserve the perfect archive or its
contradictory living witnesses. The canonical outcome breaks the index, but
player choices determine which fragments reach later ages.

## 22. Mission and object seeds

- Thirteen-Step Orrery — reveals sponsors hidden behind forecast models.
- Witness Stage — lets testimony revise an automated targeting label.
- Copper Memory Channel — fast and inspectable, but propagates corrupted edits.
- Tide Lock Commons — protects districts only while workers retain override.
- Fatta Anchor — balance mechanism that becomes a gravitational weapon.
- Ground Resonator — safely releases load or redirects it beneath an enemy.
- Climate Mirror Field — grows food before military conversion.
- Star-Fire Chamber — construction tool with a catastrophic overload state.
- Empty-Measure Index — strengthens coordinated defence by deleting exceptions.
- Waterlogged Archive — preserves fragments whose contradictions grant new
  actions rather than penalties.

## 23. Visual foundation

- Antlan is an asymmetric federation of seven handmade punchboard rings, not a
  generic golden Atlantis.
- Copper channels, water-silver joints and replaceable witness cards expose how
  law and infrastructure connect.
- Fatta is a scarred imported moon assembly with Deya repairs and thirteen
  visible route stations.
- Arey communities use dark mineral card, sealed pressure fabrics and repaired
  atmosphere membranes, never a caricature of aliens.
- Strategic weapons remain visibly converted civic machinery.
- Nuclear damage appears as scorched card edges, fused varnish and glass-like
  inserts, not realistic spectacle or celebratory explosions.
- The drowning progresses ring by ring; objects retain waterlogged labels and
  conflicting evacuation arrows.

Gameplay objects remain exact top-down orthographic 90 degrees in premium
handmade cardboard style. No swastika-derived ornament, supremacist imagery,
generic crystal sci-fi or pseudo-Greek Atlantis pastiche.

## 24. Canon boundaries

Canon inside the fictional world:

- the Ants are mixed route communities, not a pure biological race;
- Antlan is a technologically advanced island federation;
- Fatta came from the damaged Deya system;
- Arey and Deya refugees materially shape Midgard civilisation;
- Net-Miru exists, but the scale of its threat remains unresolved;
- the League attempts to use Fatta as a gravitational lever;
- both Antlan and continental states use strategic weapons;
- simultaneous commands break Fatta;
- impacts, infrastructure failure and civil war drown Antlan;
- the perfect archive is deliberately fragmented to protect living testimony;
- later histories turn a civil war into an external racial invasion.

Not canon:

- Antlan as archaeological proof of real Atlantis;
- the Ants as ancestors of one real nation;
- real ethnic or religious groups as Grey agents;
- racial mixture as the cause of decline;
- the number thirteen as objective evidence of Fatta;
- every ancient glass field, desert or flood myth as nuclear-war evidence;
- one global flood or one nuclear war explaining all ruins;
- technological power as proof of moral or racial superiority.

## 25. Bridge to the fourth age

After Antlan, surviving states inherit ruins without shared provenance. The
next age must cover:

- incompatible versions of the Veles Fragments;
- genealogists turning civic lineages into racial doctrine;
- rulers assembling a detailed pantheon from sparse records;
- the Great Race myth becoming a modern political religion;
- Greys and serpent peoples converted into universal conspirators;
- multiple cosmic wars compressed into one ancient nuclear apocalypse;
- the eventual rediscovery of buried and rebuilt cities;
- the Editor's attempt to force every fragment into one total chronology.
