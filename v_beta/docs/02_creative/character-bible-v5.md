# Ether Frontier — Character Bible v5

Status: **canonical**  
Updated: 2026-07-24

## The Binder

Role: player viewpoint and tactical authority.

Can:

- read the Bearing Ledger;
- bind temporary structures;
- assign resources and one active hero;
- accept or reject uncertain interpretations.

Cannot:

- create material without obligation;
- speak for every Road;
- move the Last Knot;
- make a copied form authentic by decree.

Arc: from efficient commander to custodian of plural responsibility.

## Rada Sveden

Role: provenance keeper, narrator and codex interface.

Starting belief: one authentic master text will restore the world.

Conflict: her increasingly successful unified interpretation also helps the
Grey Editor identify everything that does not fit.

Arc: learns that uncertainty requires disciplined evidence, not passive
relativism.

Visual:

- practical layered field coat;
- separate witness, evidence and use cards;
- graphite, burgundy and faded blue;
- no priestess costume.

## Zema

Role: reader of living ground and economy tutorial voice.

Function:

- explains cell condition, regrowth and extraction;
- warns when the defence harms the substrate;
- distinguishes material return from instant refund.

Visual:

- soil, root and water measurement cards;
- broad simple silhouette;
- repair apron and detachable pressure strips;
- not a literal personification of all Earth traditions.

## Mara

Role: keeper of lawful ending.

Function:

- closes repeating Nav loops;
- unbinds obsolete forms;
- turns debris into remembered material;
- challenges endless defence as a refusal to finish.

Visual:

- winter flax, berry-black paper, clean gaps and closure thread;
- quiet top-down silhouette;
- no skeleton queen or generic witch.

## Borislav — runtime `paladin`

Personality: direct, dependable, dangerously comfortable with command.

Combat:

- shield charges;
- close field attack;
- larger wave payment through organised service.

Arc: protection must remain revocable.

## Ayana — runtime `mage`

Personality: precise, curious, unwilling to confuse usefulness with proof.

Combat:

- global slow;
- one shield;
- stronger worksite output;
- longer Wind Watch sight.

Arc: interpretation becomes ethical when its limits stay visible.

## Amba — runtime `hunter`

Personality: economical, observant, sceptical of central maps.

Combat:

- near-base thorn pulse;
- higher ground-enemy recovery;
- stronger first tower answer.

Arc: a path is maintained by passage, not ownership.

## Sarma

Role: Lower-Serpent cartographer and political counter-witness.

Function:

- distinguishes Depths, Nav and Grey Interval;
- reveals coerced serpent units;
- provides pressure/heat route knowledge.

Arc: moves from envoy protecting one treaty house to negotiator of the
distributed covenant.

## Grey Editor

Role: antagonist voice generated by distributed Grey instances.

Method:

1. preserve a true fragment;
2. remove its uncertainty;
3. connect it to every other fragment;
4. call disagreement corruption;
5. replace people with compliant categories.

Visual:

- never one racialised monster;
- humanlike carrier remains restrained;
- separate correction tools perform the threat.
