# Ether Frontier — Asset Prompt Bible v7

Status: **canonical lore-first prompt framework**  
Updated: 2026-07-26

## 1. Reusable base prompt

> Production source art for Ether Frontier: Seven Circles of Iriy. Exact true
> top-down orthographic 90-degree camera. Premium handmade tabletop object made
> from separate matte printed-paper, fibre, thin wood-card, matte metal-paper
> and smooth solid punchboard pieces. Every physical child has a complete
> die-cut perimeter, warm cardboard edge, small gap and compact local contact
> shadow. Stable footprint, pivot and attachment geometry. Moderately cartooned
> proportions and immediate mobile-scale readability. One coherent object or
> one explicitly requested state family with generous safe margins.

This visual prefix contains no inherited weapon, role, palette or ability.

## 2. Reusable negative prompt

> No isometric or tilted view, facade, side view, horizon, glossy 3D, plastic,
> photorealism, corrugated cardboard, fused children, labels, grid, pivot marks,
> fake alphabet, generic runes, copied sacred or ethnic emblems, Soviet state
> symbols, propaganda, racial caricature, clipping or watermark. Do not import
> a bow, crossbow, ballista, cannon, gun, mortar, ammunition, projectile,
> elemental spell, crystal, fantasy class or old silhouette unless the current
> lore-first brief proves that component.

## 3. Required lore-first brief

Every per-object prompt defines, in order:

1. runtime handle, if one exists;
2. Circle and lore question;
3. gameplay verb, affected relationship and revocation condition;
4. material process that performs the verb;
5. stable footprint;
6. independent children and attachment ownership;
7. neutral, active, resolved, interrupted, captured and destroyed states;
8. directional/rotational states required by runtime;
9. emitted cards, threads, gaps, pressure pieces or flow strips;
10. forbidden inheritance from the previous object at that runtime ID.

If items 2–4 are missing, generation is forbidden.

## 4. Animation source contract

- `neutral`: readable complete assembly;
- `listen`: samples Road, owner, load, flow or witness state;
- `prepare`: children move into mechanically plausible alignment;
- `commit`: the lore verb changes a relationship;
- `resolve`: temporary children detach, return or become spent;
- `interrupted`: action stops without changing base geometry;
- `captured`: owner child is absent; removable Grey directives are added;
- `destroyed`: material-specific pieces separate without generic explosion.

Runtime/manifest data owns rotation, extension, fill amount, attachment
visibility and layer replacement. Prompts supply compatible states, never a
labelled atlas or baked motion path.

## 5. Seven object cores

### Wind Route Table — `watchtower`

> Low asymmetric bearing table with removable fork cards, four sampling vanes,
> tension ribbons and one owner route tab. It observes the next route choice,
> rotates a fork card and briefly redirects or delays a limited group. Animate
> sampling, rotation, fork selection, route-tab transfer and return. No
> crossbow, bolt, rack, sight, target marker or attack.

### Root Return Nursery — `sawmill`

> Low grafting table around a living cell, with seed cards, separate root loops,
> repair ties, moisture felt and one scar record. It restores an exhausted cell
> or grows a temporary binding root across a Road. Animate extension, taking
> hold, release, regrowth and retained scar. No saw, blade, lumber mill,
> industrial extraction or frost.

### Stone Load Chorus — `quarry`

> Open ring of non-identical witness slabs connected by load cords,
> counterweights and pressure seats. It anchors a cell and redistributes load
> until a heavy or captured unit exposes a detachable layer. Animate weighing,
> tension, settling, transfer, crack and reseating. No mine, derrick,
> projectile, lightning or artillery.

### Water Exchange Weir — `palisade`

> Shallow removable paper channels, exchange cups, silver flow strips and
> return gates around an open centre. It divides movement into delayed and
> returned steps and washes one imposed layer from a nearby friendly object.
> Animate filling, division, carrying, return and drying. No wall, stakes,
> thorns, trap, poison or ice spell.

### Name Witness Table — `obelisk`

> Low worktable with an absent centre, non-identical provenance tabs, owner
> sockets, dispute clips and one revocation thread. It identifies who owns an
> action, prevents duplication and releases a captured object after witness
> agreement. Animate witnessing, attachment, disagreement, revocation and tab
> return. No monument, void attack, rune, archive stamp or crystal.

### Sun Comparison Measure — `beacon`

> Low amber comparison surface with independent shutters, colour samples,
> exposure cards and peel tabs. It reveals copied directives and temporarily
> restores the original layer beneath an overwrite. Animate opening,
> comparison, exposure, directive peel and closure. No holy symbol, anti-air
> beam, sun projectile, lighthouse or spire.

### Thunder Answer Drum — `cannon`

> Low ring of non-identical contact drums joined by removable copper bridges
> and timed clappers. It stores contributions from nearby different Circles and
> interrupts one hostile action only when timings align. Animate contribution,
> bridge alignment, shared strike, interruption-token release and cooling. No
> barrel, cannon, shell, explosion, lightning tower or solitary trigger.

## 6. Combination rule

Specializations compose two Circle verbs. The prompt shows which children
belong to each Circle and which temporary bridge performs the combination. No
weapon upgrade trees and no merely larger versions.

Approved combinations: Wind+Root living detour; Wind+Stone heavy route;
Wind+Name copied-route distinction; Stone+Thunder load interruption;
Thunder+Water distributed answer; Sun+Name owner return; Root+Water cell
recovery.

## 7. Hero prompt cores

### Borislav

> Broad ochre-red worker-defender carrying three revocable copper Answer plates
> and a practical alignment mallet. He lends plates to three different Circle
> sockets; their shared return cancels one hostile action. Animate lend, align,
> shared answer, return and exhaustion. No melee attack, knight armour or holy
> shield.

### Ayana

> Indigo field interpreter with provenance cards, comparison windows and
> water-silver strips. Her field requires each step to show its source; copied
> steps take longer and captured layers become selectable. Animate opening,
> comparison, disputed step, selected layer and closure. No generic spell,
> global ice slow, staff or wizard robe.

### Amba

> Pine-and-berry route keeper with path ribbons, witness pegs and a curved
> tension tool used to place them. She creates a temporary witnessed route;
> enemies crossing it expose their obligation. Animate route laying, peg
> placement, passage, reveal and retrieval. No arrow attack, critical shot,
> falcon emblem or hunter costume.

## 8. Enemy prompt rule

Begin with the relationship the unit changes, never with `fast`, `tank`,
`bomber`, `mage` or another genre class. Then derive motion, silhouette and
layers from active faction material grammar:

- debt/pressure/treaty for Lower-Serpent groups;
- incomplete return and recoverable owner gaps for Nav;
- duplicated directives and erased provenance for Greys;
- original chassis plus detachable directives for captured machines.
