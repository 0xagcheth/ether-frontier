## Watchtower projection master

**Object:** `watchtower`  
**Display name:** Ветровой дозор  
**Primary circle:** Ветра  
**Secondary circle:** Грома  
**Role:** ранняя дальняя защита

> One assembled Ether Frontier Wind Watch / “Ветровой дозор”: a compact low
> irregular circular lookout made from twenty separate cold-grey stone-card
> tiles over a dark radial timber-card deck, with exactly two opposite access
> notches. One enlarged but simple lightweight wooden crossbow rotates on a
> layered central bearing, directly centered over and visibly attached to that
> bearing; the bearing may not remain empty and the weapon may not sit beside
> it. The crossbow is the strongest cartoon color and
> silhouette accent but remains clearly lighter than a Ballista. Add one muted
> burgundy radial signal-cloth card with a short stepped edge rhythm, one small
> amber lantern, and one low rack of four bolts plus one empty slot.
>
> Seven-Circles lore: one restrained broken sky-blue path mark for the Circle of
> Wind, four simple copper fastening arcs for the Circle of Thunder, hand-cut
> craft notches and a faded sky-blue repair card. No archive stamp, no
> Kitezh-17 geometry, no laboratory enamel, no readable text. Every height step
> and functional component reads as an independent solid-cardboard cutout.
