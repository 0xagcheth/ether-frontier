# Ether Frontier — Asset Prompt Bible v6

Status: **archival weapon-led prompt language; superseded by v7**  
Updated: 2026-07-24

## 1. Universal ImageGen 2 master prefix

> Production source art for Ether Frontier: Seven Circles of Iriy — Seven
> Roads of the Last Knot, a
> stylized tower-defence board game. Exact true top-down orthographic
> 90-degree camera for gameplay objects. Premium handmade tabletop diorama
> assembled from many separate smooth solid punchboard, printed paper, fibre,
> thin wood-card and occasional matte metal-paper pieces. Every physical part
> has its own complete die-cut perimeter, visible tan cardboard edge, small
> separation gap and compact under-edge contact shadow. Appealing slightly
> cartooned proportions; the primary gameplay mechanism is the largest,
> clearest and most saturated feature. Warm earthy print colours, visible paper
> grain, restrained ink misregistration and a few hand repair marks. Lore
> appears through material process, construction and function, never through a
> collage of copied sacred signs.

## 2. Universal negative prompt

> No glossy 3D render, no plastic miniature, no photorealism, no facade, no
> side or rear view, no isometric camera, no tall perspective, no baked terrain,
> no labels, no grid, no pivot crosses, no assembled preview inside runtime
> source, no fake alphabet, no generic runes, no copied religious or ethnic
> emblem, no Soviet state symbol, no propaganda slogan, no racial caricature,
> no universal crossbow, no clipping, no fused moving parts.

## 3. Required prompt fields

Every call must state:

1. runtime ID and canon display name;
2. runtime role and target class;
3. lore owner/process;
4. stable footprint;
5. enlarged primary mechanism;
6. all independent moving/animated children;
7. projectile, impact and debris family;
8. exact projection;
9. cultural source lane and transformation distance, if any;
10. forbidden motifs from related objects.

## 4. Environment concept prompt

> A real gameplay-view concept for the Last Knot: portrait tower-defence board
> seen from exact overhead, two ochre Roads enter from the north, cross,
> separate and rejoin at a low seven-socket central Knot. Dense but readable
> forest and memory-stone resource cells surround the paths. Several separate
> cardboard towers show clearly different mechanisms: a small Wind Watch
> crossbow, a large anti-air ballista, a dark Thunder cannon, an amber Sun
> reflector, a root spring launcher, a toothed Carpenter Circle and a pulley
> Stone Circle. Warm board-game print, playful readable silhouettes, visible
> individual punchboard layers. Buried Antlan water channels and older depth
> pressure marks appear only as subtle exposed strata. Pale Grey units, warm
> clay serpent units and torn translucent Nav forms are visibly separate enemy
> grammars. UI-free game concept art, not a poster.

## 5. Runtime tower prompts

### `watchtower` — Wind Watch

> Low irregular twenty-stone card ring and eight-part radial timber deck. One
> modest lightweight rotating crossbow centered on a layered bearing: body,
> left arm, right arm, tense and released string and loaded bolt are separate.
> Add one sky-blue route card, four copper bearing contacts, separate wind cloth,
> lantern casing, flame, glow and four-bolt rack. The crossbow is clear but
> smaller than every dedicated ballista. No facade tower, no oversized siege
> bow, no symbols.

### `ranger` — Wind Ranger

> Same family footprint grammar but a unique compact rapid launcher: two short
> limbs, sliding feed rail, small bolt magazine and ranging vane. Separate
> acquisition ring, feed, bolt and wind marker. No Watchtower recolour, no
> firearm.

### `tracker` — Falcon Route

> Low open sighting platform with rotating path frame, precision non-crossbow
> bow stave, separate bird marker and paper wind ribbons. Long-range function
> dominates. No literal sacred bird emblem and no sniper rifle.

### `assassin` — Silent Trace

> Low concealed torsion-dart bed with narrow rails, tension tabs and independent
> shutter cloth. One heavy dart and silent fibre impact. No crossbow silhouette,
> no gun and no assassin costume icon.

### `ballista` — Thunder Ballista

> Heavy low tension base with enormous separate arms, reinforced central stock,
> winch, tension ropes, recoil slide and one large anti-air bolt. Copper Thunder
> contacts are functional fasteners. No Wind Watch proportions.

### `scorpion` — Thunder Harpoon

> Single straight harpoon rail, segmented counterweight tail, large rope drum,
> separate cable, hook and return states. No paired ballista arms.

### `hailstorm` — Stribog Volley

> Fan of multiple short launch channels, rotating feed drum and numerous small
> loaded darts. Wide airy silhouette. No giant single bow.

### `cannon` — Thunder Engine

> Low circular carriage, dark matte barrel card, separate breech, copper braces,
> recoil rails, round charge, muzzle flash and compact smoke. No bow parts.

### `mortar` — Bear Mortar

> Short wide bowl-like tube in an elevation cradle, rotating socket and heavy
> shell. Separate arc marker and broad ground impact. No cannon-length barrel.

### `grapeshot` — Sevenfold Roll

> Seven short guide channels around one trigger plate, seven charge tabs and
> wide pellet fan. Readable top-down cone function. No single cannon barrel.

### `palisade` — Root Weave

> Living stakes and root sockets forming a low open boundary. Independent
> spring roots and thorn launch tabs provide rapid anti-ground fire. Separate
> closure vines, damage states, splinters and leaf debris. No crossbow.

### `obelisk` — Name Stone

> Low stepped memory slabs around an unmistakable absent centre. Independent
> name-bridge cards rotate to expose captured-machine directives. Copper bridges
> and violet-grey interruption pulse. No tall monument facade or crystal.

### `beacon` — Sun Measure

> Low amber lens on a radial reflector with separate ochre shutter petals, beam
> cards and glow rings. Long-range air revelation. No tower spire or generic
> holy sun symbol.

### `sawmill` — Carpenter Circle

> Radial workbench with exaggerated toothed saw wheel, separate belt, small
> gears, log bed, wood stacks, shavings and repair cards. The saw is the main
> cartoon accent. No building facade.

### `quarry` — Stone Circle

> Open low cut-stone worksite, compact derrick, rotating pulley arm, rope, basket,
> sorted slabs, rubble and dust. The lifting mechanism dominates. No mine
> entrance facade.

## 7. Enemy prompt grammar

### Lower-Serpent

> Warm clay-card bodies, shed-scale armour, pressure-fit stone, underground
> water and clan-specific obligation plates. Individuals have readable tools
> and roles. No conspiracy-luxury coding, no real ethnic features, no universal
> evil palette.

### Nav

> Torn translucent paper, incomplete contours, missing but recoverable name
> tabs and loose threads. Melancholy and unstable, not generic undead gore.

### Greys

> Pale repeated correction cards, erased labels, too-perfect duplicated
> geometry and cold gaps where owner-specific layers should be. No reference to
> a human ethnicity.

### Captured machines

> A colourful locally made craft chassis remains visible beneath removable
> pale Grey directive plates. Capture reads as loss of unique layers, not a full
> recolour.

## 9. Source-generation and atlas rules

- ImageGen 2 by default.
- Generate one coherent projection master before decomposition.
- Complex state edits must preserve the same geometry.
- Never request the whole labelled atlas from ImageGen.
- Chroma-to-alpha, layer extraction, pivot assignment and packing are
  deterministic pipeline stages.
- One family per atlas, maximum 5 MB.
- Runtime atlas contains only tight transparent sprites.
- Review grid and composite are separate QA files.
- Manifest JSON owns rects, pivots, attachments, draw order and animations.
- No next object until visual and technical approval.
