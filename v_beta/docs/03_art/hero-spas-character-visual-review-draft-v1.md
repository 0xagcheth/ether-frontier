# Ether Frontier — Hero: Spas Character and Visual Direction v1

Status: **canonical character and visual direction; approved 2026-07-26**  
Updated: 2026-07-26

## Approved scope

This document canonically defines only:

- Spas's place in the fictional version of the myth;
- personality and dramatic contradiction;
- in-world appearance;
- static gameplay silhouette.

It does not approve abilities, combat role, animation states, prompt text or
generated art. Those require separate staged review.

## 1. Source position

In the pseudo-historical corpus, Spas is the priest who foresees that the
fragments of destroyed Lelya will fall upon Midgard and that Daariya must be
abandoned. The warning begins the migration south along the Riphean Mountains.

The game preserves this role but does not confirm supernatural clairvoyance as
the only explanation.

## 2. Adapted identity

**Spas is a title, not his birth name.**

The title belongs to the senior Keeper of Imminent Passage in Daariya: a person
responsible for recognising when a settlement can no longer be defended and
for opening evacuation routes before the authorities admit defeat.

The current Spas has held the office for twenty-three years. Until the Lelya
crisis, he has never ordered a full evacuation. Most citizens therefore regard
his office as ceremonial—an old institution maintained because Daariya claims
to remember every previous catastrophe.

He has no canonical personal name. Everyone addresses him by office, allowing
later chronicles to transform a fallible person into a timeless prophetic
figure.

## 3. Origin

Spas was born in the Kh'Aryan Measure district but spent his working life among
all four Daariyan territories.

He began as a lunar surveyor:

- comparing the movement of Lelya, Fatta and Mesyats;
- recording pressure changes in the four rivers;
- maintaining warning stones along the Northern Well;
- learning oral forecast methods from Svyatorus choirs;
- exchanging underground load records with Naga route-keepers.

He received the title Spas not because he was the wisest priest, but because he
was the only candidate willing to include Lower Passage measurements in the
official polar model.

## 4. What he discovers

Spas compares several systems that normally contradict one another:

- lunar mechanics;
- Daariyan sacred calendar;
- pressure changes beneath the polar continent;
- Lelya reservoir reports;
- Naga water-route testimony;
- irregular signals from the occupied moon.

None provides an exact future. All point to the same conclusion: if Dazhbog
strikes Lelya, Daariya will be destroyed by the consequences.

Later chronicles call this a divine vision because “the priest saw the flood”
is easier to preserve than the disagreement of six incomplete models.

## 5. Character

### Outward behaviour

- speaks slowly and concretely;
- never raises his voice to imitate authority;
- asks who measured something and what they could not see;
- touches stone, water and machinery while thinking;
- writes dates only after naming the calendar;
- treats panic as information rather than moral weakness.

### Inner contradiction

Spas is exceptionally good at recognising when something must end. He is poor
at accepting that people may refuse to follow him.

He believes his forecast gives him the right to decide the order of evacuation.
During the migration he must learn that saving people does not make their
consent irrelevant.

### Fear

His deepest fear is not being wrong. It is being correct too early, ordering a
needless exodus and becoming the man who destroyed Daariya without a flood.

### Dry humour

Spas does not make jokes deliberately. His humour comes from literal answers:

> “The Council says the moon cannot fall.”  
> “The Council has no authority over the moon.”

## 6. Age and physique

- apparent age: approximately 55–60;
- tall, broad-backed and physically durable from survey travel;
- neither frail sage nor muscular warrior;
- slightly uneven shoulders from carrying a lunar instrument for decades;
- heavy hands marked by stone dust, cold burns and copper oxidation;
- movements economical, with no ceremonial flourish.

He must look capable of walking at the front of an evacuation column for many
days, not merely issuing prophecy from a temple.

## 7. Face

- long rectangular face with a heavy brow and tired, attentive eyes;
- weathered polar skin rather than idealised porcelain whiteness;
- dark ash-brown hair, strongly grey at temples;
- short practical beard, unevenly cut during the evacuation;
- one pale pressure scar crossing the left eyebrow;
- no glowing eyes, divine mark, royal beauty or racial-ideal features.

His expression is usually concentrated rather than stern. The essential look
is a man listening to a structure that everyone else has declared silent.

## 8. Clothing

Spas wears the field clothing of a monumental stone civilisation, not fantasy
priest robes.

### Base layer

A dark indigo, knee-length survey coat made from tightly woven cold-resistant
cloth. Broad enough for movement, reinforced at shoulder and hip.

### Stone mantle

Across the shoulders lies a segmented pale stone mantle consisting of four
large plates. Each plate comes from one Daariyan district and has a different
edge treatment. Together they almost form a circle but leave a deliberate gap
behind the right shoulder.

The mantle is ceremonial and functional: its plates act as reference weights
for pressure instruments. It must not resemble armour.

### River sash

A wide dark-turquoise sash crosses the torso and divides into four narrow ends
at the belt, representing the four engineered rivers. Real wear and water marks
make the symbolism secondary to use.

### Lower clothing

Heavy grey-blue trousers, wrapped cold-weather boots and a split outer apron
holding measurement leaves. No flowing hem that would obscure the top-down leg
silhouette.

## 9. Equipment

### Three-Moon Measure

Spas's main visual identifier is a folding astronomical instrument carried over
his left shoulder.

It consists of:

- one large dark ring representing Fatta;
- one small dense ring representing Lelya;
- one calm silver arc representing Mesyats;
- a central plumb weight aligned to Midgard;
- replaceable copper joints showing many repairs.

It is neither a magical halo nor a conventional weapon. In the folded position,
its three unequal circles create an immediately readable silhouette from above.
After the destruction of Lelya, one small recovered fragment is secured inside
the dense Lelya ring and can be released through the instrument as Spas's
approved ordinary attack. The fragment does not change the static silhouette.

### Warning leaves

Six thick ceramic-and-metal record leaves hang separately from his belt. They
represent the six incompatible models behind the evacuation forecast. Their
edges and fastening systems must visibly differ.

### Route cord

A long ochre evacuation cord is wrapped around his right forearm. It is marked
with physical knots and removable location pieces rather than written runes.

### Northern Well fragment

He carries one broken piece of a polar warning stone. It proves the underground
pressure system reacted before the official lunar instruments.

## 10. Palette

- dominant: cold blue-grey stone;
- body field: deep indigo;
- secondary: dark river turquoise;
- structural metal: oxidised copper;
- route accent: restrained warm ochre;
- single warning accent: dull mineral red on one Lelya record leaf;
- hair and leather: ash-brown and charcoal.

No pure white-gold saint palette and no red military-command palette.

## 11. Monumental-world presentation

In narrative concept art, Spas appears small against enormous Daariyan stone
structures. He is visually framed by:

- the three moons above the Northern Well;
- four river channels converging under observation platforms;
- evacuation markers beginning to extend south;
- officials standing beneath a monumental calendar that shows a safer date.

The image must communicate that Spas is challenging not one villain but the
physical and institutional weight of the entire civilisation.

## 12. Gameplay-object silhouette

The gameplay representation uses premium matte punchboard, while depicting the
same in-world materials.

From exact top-down view, Spas is identified by five large shapes:

1. four-part pale shoulder mantle;
2. off-centre three-ring lunar instrument on the left;
3. turquoise diagonal river sash;
4. six separate record leaves at the belt;
5. ochre route cord around the right arm.

His footprint is taller than wide. The lunar instrument creates left-side
weight; the cord and hanging leaves balance it without producing symmetry.

## 13. Static pose for first concept

Spas stands on a monumental lunar observation platform. His body faces the
Northern Well, but his head turns toward the first evacuation column.

The Three-Moon Measure is half-open. One hand holds the plumb weight above a
cracked pressure line. The other has just released the ochre route cord, which
extends south across the stone floor.

He is not posing heroically. The decisive visual moment is that the evacuation
cord crosses the official ceremonial boundary before anyone has authorised it.

## 14. Explicit exclusions

- no generic wizard, druid, monk or Orthodox-priest silhouette;
- no staff, wand, sword, shield or war hammer;
- no antler crown, horned helmet or fur barbarian clothing;
- no glowing runes, magic circle or floating scripture;
- no idealised white-race appearance;
- no elderly helpless prophet supported by attendants;
- no royal crown or divine halo;
- no cardboard appearance in narrative world art;
- no ability effects or animation frames in the first concept.

## 15. Approval result

Approved without reservation:

- Spas remains a title and no personal name is assigned;
- apparent age remains 55–60;
- the four-part stone mantle remains;
- the Three-Moon Measure remains his defining visual object;
- his central flaw is deciding for others because his forecast is correct.
