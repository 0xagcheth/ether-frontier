# VFX Style Guide

## Purpose

Effects identify cause, faction, target and damage type. Every layered-object
effect is an independent die-cut paper token or frame family.

## Zastava Effects

- memory/restoration: amber, ochre and warm white paper glows;
- Gromovik: compact cyan-white lightning with copper contact sparks;
- Archive signs: blue and birch-tan rings, labels and broken text fragments;
- Pathfinder: pine green vines, river-blue traces, leaves and feather markers.

## Enemy Effects

- Lower City: clay dust, splinters, marsh-green venom and dark turquoise scale;
- Grey: thin cold-cyan lines, pale lens pulses and silent geometric cuts;
- damaged Nav: incomplete indigo contours, smoke-white paper curls and missing
  center shapes;
- machines: enamel chips, copper sparks, short grey smoke cards and cable debris.

## Projectile Rules

- projectiles remain readable at gameplay scale;
- trails are short and owner-specific;
- a projectile never hides its target for more than a brief impact;
- directional projectiles match their owning unit projection.

## Impact Rules

- bolt: sharp contact and tiny fibers/splinters;
- Grom-battery or mortar: compact smoke, enamel chips and a short shock ring;
- Dazhbog circle: clean ochre-gold radial flash;
- Veles stone: interrupted machine contour and archive-sign fracture;
- Grey attack: precise pale cut without generic purple magic bloom.

## Forbidden

- realistic particles, volumetric smoke or transparent flame;
- large soft bloom clouds;
- persistent glow that hides silhouettes;
- deprecated Horde toxic-green identity;
- full-screen runes or copied historical sacred symbols.
