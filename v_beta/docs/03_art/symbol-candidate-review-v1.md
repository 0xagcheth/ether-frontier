## Required v2 changes

### Fatta

Remove circular rotational rhythm. Use two offset orbital rails rather than
near-complete rings. Keep three unequal bridge blocks, but no bridge may imply a
hook or direction of spin.

### Lower City

Replace continuous spiral with three nested shell plates around a displaced
service opening. Keep one technical outer tooth.

### Grey

Replace eye outline with an incomplete ceramic lens assembly: two unequal shutter
plates around an offset slit. No almond shape.

### Kitezh-17

Remove all crosshair segments. Use an offset circular site punch intersected by
one archive tab and one inventory corner, not a centered optical symbol.

### Other signs

Increase object-specific asymmetry and reduce resemblance to generic UI rings.
Lelya must not form a face at 16–32 px. Month requires a clearly archival
connector rather than a decorative dot.

## Gate

External image-similarity and trademark search should use v2 or later candidates,
not the rejected v1 forms.
