# Ether Frontier — World Concept Art Prompt Bible v1

Status: **canonical pre-atlas concept prompt layer**  
Updated: 2026-07-24

## Purpose

Этот документ управляет только concept art мира. Концепты фиксируют настроение,
материал, масштаб, цвет, фракции и читаемость игрового поля. Они не являются
sprite sheets и не задают точные rects, pivots или layer geometry.

## Universal ImageGen prefix

> Ether Frontier: Seven Circles of Iriy, an original mythic tower-defense world
> presented as a premium handcrafted tabletop diorama made from separately
> die-cut smooth cardboard and printed paper. Visible fine paper fibers, thin tan
> punchboard edges, tiny separation gaps and compact contact shadows between
> physical layers. Charming restrained cartoon illustration printed on every
> piece, large readable silhouettes, warm clear earthy inks, cool shadows and
> warm amber memory light. Ancient Rus craft construction, Far Eastern taiga and
> restrained late-20th-century engineering rhythm coexist as distinct material
> memories without copied sacred signs, state emblems, logos or recognizable
> characters.

## Lore lock

Every concept must show or imply:

- the last active Seventh Circle guarding the road between Yav and Iriy;
- the Great Forest and one or more extinguished circles;
- defenders as living craft agreements, not generic factory turrets;
- enemies as Children of the Lower Serpent, Greys, damaged Nav echoes or
  captured constructions;
- the road opening in waves as the tower-defense cause;
- difference, name and memory opposed to Grey erasure.

Never include Kitezh-17, a secret lab, archive stamps, controlling code,
Goblin Horde, Grimhold, generic Western fantasy heraldry or copied Slavic
symbols.

## Material lock

- smooth solid punchboard, never corrugated packaging;
- all important components are separate physical cutouts;
- printed color retains visible paper grain;
- low compact cardboard depth;
- no glossy 3D, plastic, clay or photoreal miniatures;
- no single flat illustration pretending to be layered;
- no excessive beige wash;
- no giant cast shadows on the tabletop background.

## Concept set

### C01 — Gameplay vision / The Seventh Circle

Use case: `stylized-concept`  
Aspect: wide 16:9  
Camera: 80–90° overhead gameplay-board view.

Show one complete tower-defense battlefield: a pale winding memory road across
dark blue-green taiga, the active Seventh Circle as the base objective, several
small distinct craft defenses belonging to different circles, and readable
enemy waves approaching from an extinguished gate. Include a cold river edge,
sopka stone, layered paper trees, one faded engineering platform and fragments
of the star road. The scene must immediately communicate path, build zones,
defenders, enemies and objective.

### C02 — Key art / The road between Yav and Iriy

Use case: `illustration-story`  
Aspect: wide 16:9  
Camera: expressive diorama presentation, still visibly physical cardboard.

Show the Knowing One at the Seventh Circle restoring names while the star road
opens above layered taiga. In the middle distance, the Thunderer and forest
spirits hold back the Children of the Lower Serpent; smooth pale Grey cards
erase color and seams from the far side. Beneath cracked terrain layers, the
outline of the World Serpent egg is implied, not fully revealed. The emotional
focus is fragile shared memory, not triumphalist conquest.

### C03 — Faction and material confrontation

Use case: `stylized-concept`  
Aspect: wide 16:9 triptych without labels.

Left: Guardians and living craft objects with timber, birch bark, stone,
burgundy, pine green, sky blue and amber. Center: Children of the Lower Serpent
with clay, layered stone, bone, shell cards, marsh green and dark turquoise.
Right: Greys as smooth ash and pale-lilac overlays closing seams and replacing
distinct pieces with anonymous repeated modules. All remain original cardboard
designs; Greys are not human ethnic imagery.

### C04 — Material close-up / Living tabletop

Use case: `product-mockup`  
Aspect: 4:3 close review board.

Show enlarged neutral fragments from all seven circles as separate unlabeled
die-cut samples: root weave, stone bond, copper thunder fastening, solar
aperture, wind cloth/path, water ripple/bridge and mismatched Name cards. This
image establishes fibers, print grain, cut edges, layer gaps, contact shadows,
color density and ornament restraint. No complete gameplay object or reusable
weapon silhouette.

## Shared avoid block

> Avoid: glossy CGI, 3D-rendered miniatures, plastic tokens, clay, corrugated
> cardboard, photoreal landscape, painterly canvas, flat vector infographic,
> isometric videogame screenshot, facade-dominant buildings, generic medieval
> castle, generic Viking imagery, copied runes or sacred symbols, Soviet emblems,
> laboratory signs, labels, text, watermark, visual clutter and duplicated
> objects.

## Approval

Concepts are approved independently:

1. C04 locks material.
2. C01 locks gameplay camera and field readability.
3. C02 locks emotional world identity.
4. C03 locks faction separation.

Only the rules common to approved concepts may be transferred into production
atlas prompts.
