# Watchtower Projection Master Review v2

Updated: 2026-07-23

Status: **REJECTED**

Rejection reason: the cardboard construction is clearer, but the surface
language remains generic fantasy punchboard and does not carry enough
Kitezh-17 / Last Outpost of Yav identity.

## Correction applied

V2 replaces the continuous illustrated-object language with an assembled
tabletop punchboard construction:

- every major component has a closed die-cut perimeter;
- warm solid-cardboard edges remain visible;
- stacked pieces have compact contact shadows;
- neighboring elements use physical separation gaps;
- stone ring tiles read as individual cardboard pieces;
- deck, bearing, crossbow, signal cloth, lantern and bolt reserve are visually
  independent modules;
- surfaces use matte printed paper rather than realistic material rendering.
