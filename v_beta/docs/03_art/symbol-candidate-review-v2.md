## Prompt intent

One monochrome 2×2 structural sheet:

1. Fatta as two offset open orbital rails with three unequal bridges;
2. Lower City as nested shell plates around an off-center service opening;
3. Grey as unequal ceramic shutters around an offset slit;
4. Kitezh-17 as archive frame with side tab and offset site punch.

No text, texture, political/religious signs, crosshair, eye, camera or 3D.

## Review

### Fatta — top left

Improvement: unequal blocks and broken rails reduce the explicit solar-wheel
reading.

Problem: the whole silhouette remains a nearly concentric circular mechanism and
still implies rotation.

Decision: **REJECT v2**. Next candidate must abandon full circular rails and use
two offset orbital arcs with clearly different centers and lengths.

### Lower City — top right

Improvement: no continuous smooth spiral; shell construction and service opening
are readable.

Problem: outer plates still create a spiral-like flow, but the mechanical tooth
and polygonal center reduce the generic/snail association.

Decision: **PASS DIRECTION / ITERATE**. Use this as a reference direction, not a
final mark.

### Grey — bottom left

Improvement: no almond eye outline, no pupil and no camera aperture. Two unequal
ceramic shutters read as a mechanism.

Problem: at small scale the offset slit may disappear and leave a generic broken
disc.

Decision: **PASS DIRECTION / ITERATE**. Preserve unequal shutters and enlarge
the offset slit in the simplified variant.

### Kitezh-17 — bottom right

Improvement: no target or crosshair.

Problem: reads as a generic document/file/application icon with notification
circle.

Decision: **REJECT v2**. Next candidate must stop using a complete rectangular
frame. Use an open archive bracket, one physical site-punch and an inventory
corner that cannot read as a software file icon.

## Gate

- Lower City and Grey may proceed to dedicated single-symbol ImageGen 2
  iterations.
- Fatta and Kitezh-17 require a new geometry brief.
- No v2 mark enters production objects, UI, runtime atlases or branding.
