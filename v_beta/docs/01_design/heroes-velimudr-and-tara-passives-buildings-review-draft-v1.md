# Ether Frontier — Heroes: Velimudr and Tara Passives and Buildings v1

Status: **hero passives remain canonical; all legacy buildings superseded 2026-07-27**  
Updated: 2026-07-27

## Approved scope

This document canonically defines:

- one passive and three unique buildings for Velimudr;
- one passive and three unique buildings for Tara.

It does not approve animations, exact numerical balance, runtime code, prompts
or generated art.

Approved character and appearance remain unchanged.

# Part I — Velimudr

> **Superseded notice:** Velimudr's passive **Right of Revision** in this part
> remains canonical. The three buildings described below are rejected and
> replaced by `hero-velimudr-combat-buildings-revision-review-draft-v2.md`:
> **Table of Three Interpretations**, **Board of Loud Reading** and **Circle of
> Silent Witnesses**. Legacy building text is retained only for provenance.

## 1. Gameplay identity

Velimudr is a hero of **revision, attribution and adaptation**.

His kit helps the player:

- correct a recent construction mistake;
- choose between different legitimate uses of one record;
- strengthen one building by restoring missing context;
- learn from a repeated hostile action and counter later copies.

He does not predict the future like Spas. He becomes stronger after seeing how
an action was actually used.

## 2. Passive — Right of Revision

The most recent player construction, upgrade or specialisation receives a
temporary **revision tab**.

For a short interval, the player may revoke that decision:

- the previous building state is restored;
- all committed resources are returned;
- the cell and owner remain unchanged;
- no sell penalty is applied.

Only the most recent eligible decision may carry the tab.

### Restrictions

- damage taken after the decision is not undone;
- enemy movement, wave results and resource income are never rewound;
- destroyed or captured buildings cannot be restored through revision;
- a decision may be revised only once;
- hero-specific building activations are not refunded after producing an
  effect.

### Purpose

The passive expresses Velimudr's belief that interpretation must remain
revocable. It gives the player flexibility without becoming time travel.

## 3. Building I — Table of Three Versions

Hero handle: `velimudr_three_versions`  
Tier: early / inexpensive  
Placement: ordinary opened cell  
Limit: 3

### Appearance concept

A low imperfect triangular table. Its three sides hold a damaged source copy,
a confident public edition and a visibly corrected version. Copper hinges have
different lengths, so the table never becomes perfectly symmetrical.

### Ability — Select the Reading

Before each wave, the player selects one of three modes.

#### Source

Nearby buildings act more slowly but gain greater observation radius and resist
Grey capture. They prioritise enemies whose owner or origin is already known.

#### Public Edition

Nearby buildings prepare and repeat their ordinary action faster. In exchange,
their action becomes easier for Grey Instructors to copy.

#### Correction

Nearby buildings lose the speed bonus but their first successful interaction
with an imposed enemy layer weakens or removes that layer.

### Mode rule

The mode cannot be changed during a wave. All three readings are useful; none
is presented as the only true version.

### Upgrade direction

- Level 1: small area and moderate mode effects;
- Level 2: larger area;
- Level 3: one nearby building may retain the previous mode while the rest
  adopt the new reading.

### Gameplay identity

Flexible early support. The player chooses information, tempo or correction.

## 4. Building II — Workshop of Missing Context

Hero handle: `velimudr_context_workshop`  
Tier: middle / moderate cost  
Placement: opened cell beside another friendly building  
Limit: 2

### Appearance concept

A rectangular low workshop with an open central work surface, oversized copper
revision clips, several differently coloured source strips and one unfinished
wooden board.

It is not a library tower, scriptorium facade or magical archive.

### Ability — Restore the Footnote

The Workshop attaches one **context strip** to a selected nearby building.

The strip records:

- who owns the building;
- what type of action it performs;
- which target relationship it may legitimately affect;
- when the action may be revoked.

While attached, the building:

- cannot have its owner removed by the first capture attempt each wave;
- can recognise copied and disguised enemy layers appropriate to its function;
- recovers faster after being interrupted;
- cannot be duplicated by the Engine of the Empty Name.

### Limitation

Each Workshop maintains only one context strip. Moving it to another building
takes time and leaves both buildings briefly without protection.

### Upgrade direction

- Level 1: protects owner and exposes appropriate copied layers;
- Level 2: faster recovery after interruption;
- Level 3: maintains two strips, but they must be attached to different
  building families.

### Gameplay identity

Focused protection and strengthening of an important specialised building.

## 5. Building III — Unfinished Board

Hero handle: `velimudr_unfinished_board`  
Tier: late / expensive  
Placement: large opened cell with Road coverage  
Limit: 1

### Appearance concept

A monumental horizontal wooden board mounted inside a stone-and-metal comparison
frame. Its final section is physically absent. Around it sit three source
holders and a separate speaker-present token.

The empty section must remain visibly empty. No glowing missing text appears.

### Ability — Do Not Complete the Sentence

The Board records the first special enemy action completed within its area:

- capture;
- raid;
- summoning;
- copied-route opening;
- answer silencing;
- directive replacement.

The first occurrence succeeds normally. The Board preserves its exact source,
sequence and target.

Later **identical copies** of that action within range become slower and weaker:

- preparation takes longer;
- nearby buildings receive earlier warning;
- the copied action may be interrupted by an appropriate building with reduced
  requirements.

### Unknown protection

The Board never invents an explanation for an unrecognised action. A different
action occupies a new record slot rather than being forced into the first
category.

### Capacity

- Level 1: remembers one action family;
- Level 2: remembers two;
- Level 3: remembers three and fully interrupts the third identical repetition
  of any recorded action within one wave.

### Gameplay identity

Late adaptive control. It is weak against a completely new attack and powerful
against the Editor's repeated templates.

## 6. Velimudr kit relationship

| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| Right of Revision | correct one recent player decision without rewinding combat |
| Table of Three Versions | choose information, tempo or correction for a local group |
| Workshop of Missing Context | protect and strengthen one specialised building |
| Unfinished Board | learn from repeated hostile actions and weaken later copies |

### Strengths

- flexible building decisions;
- strong resistance to Grey capture and copying;
- powerful support for one key structure;
- excellent against repeated enemy abilities;
- supports different tower families rather than one optimal build.

### Weaknesses

- no passive economy gain;
- first occurrence of a new hostile action remains dangerous;
- Public Edition mode increases copying risk;
- requires player attention and deliberate mode selection;
- little effect against simple enemies using no special actions.

### Explicit exclusions

- no magical book projectiles;
- no generic glowing word spells or runes; Velimudr's canonical ordinary attack
  uses one bounded material operational formula owned by the dedicated attack
  document;
- no time rewind;
- no infinite free respecialisation;
- no structure that simply copies another structure's full ability;
- no claim that Velimudr owns the final correct edition.

# Part II — Tara

> **Superseded notice:** Tara's passive **Ground Is Never Empty** in this part
> remains canonical. The three buildings described below are rejected and
> replaced by `hero-tara-combat-buildings-revision-review-draft-v2.md`:
> **Returned-Ground Frame**, **Three Covenant Vessels** and **Garden of Many
> Debts** retain their names but now use the revised combat abilities and
> animation direction. Legacy building text is retained only for provenance.

## 7. Gameplay identity

Tara is a hero of **restoration, shared resources and terrain control**.

Her kit helps the player:

- return exhausted cells to use sooner;
- strengthen buildings placed on repaired ground;
- slow and separate enemies through water-pressure exchange;
- turn a diverse local economy into repair and sustained defence.

She does not predict like Spas or revise decisions like Velimudr. She changes
what the ground can support now.

## 8. Passive — Ground Is Never Empty

Every exhausted tree, stone or damaged build cell retains a visible recovery
state while Tara is selected.

### Mechanical effect

- natural resource cells complete regrowth sooner;
- a sold or evacuated building leaves a recoverable material obligation rather
  than a completely dead footprint;
- the first friendly building placed on a fully restored cell gains a temporary
  **settled-ground** state;
- settled-ground buildings resist forced movement and recover from interruption
  faster during their first two waves.

### Limitation

- the passive does not create new resource cells;
- it does not instantly refill resources;
- repeatedly clearing the same cell increases its recovery time;
- Grey-captured ground must be cleansed before recovery continues.

### Purpose

The passive makes Tara an economic and positional hero without granting free
materials.

## 9. Building I — Returned-Ground Frame

Hero handle: `tara_returned_ground_frame`  
Tier: early / inexpensive  
Placement: exhausted, damaged or recovering cell  
Limit: 3

### Appearance concept

A low square frame divided into four unequal test beds: living soil, flood
residue, pressure stone and recovery organisms. One side carries a black
Lelya-glass separation tool in a pale ceramic case.

### Ability — Test Before Settlement

The Frame accelerates the recovery of its cell. When recovery completes, the
player chooses one settlement result.

#### Living bed

The next resource-producing building on the cell yields more for several waves.

#### Stable bed

The next defensive or support building gains resistance to capture, tunnelling
and forced movement.

#### Open bed

The cell becomes buildable sooner and temporarily opens one adjacent damaged
cell, but grants no lasting building bonus.

### Cost

The Frame occupies the cell during treatment and produces no resources itself.
It must be removed after the ground is approved.

### Upgrade direction

- Level 1: faster recovery;
- Level 2: stronger chosen settlement result;
- Level 3: treatment also removes one Grey ground layer or flood residue.

### Gameplay identity

Early terrain investment that turns previous loss into a deliberate future
advantage.

## 10. Building II — Three Covenant Vessels

Hero handle: `tara_covenant_vessels`  
Tier: middle / moderate cost  
Placement: beside a Road or water/pressure seam  
Limit: 3

### Appearance concept

Three separate low vessels on a broad blue-grey pressure base:

- surface settlement water;
- Lower Passage water;
- mixed covenant water.

Turquoise channels join the vessels only during an authorised exchange. They
must never appear as one fantasy cauldron.

### Ability — Shared Pressure

The building collects movement pressure from enemies crossing its area and
redistributes it through one selected exchange.

#### Surface to depth

Heavy ground enemies transfer part of their load downward and move more slowly.
Nearby Stone-type buildings prepare faster against them.

#### Depth to surface

Tunnelling enemies are forced to reveal their emergence point earlier. Nearby
buildings gain time to prepare.

#### Mixed covenant

Grey overlays and copied route layers loosen while crossing the wet exchange
area. Appropriate friendly structures can remove them more easily.

### Limits

- only one exchange operates at a time;
- changing exchange drains the vessels and creates a short inactive period;
- flying enemies are affected only if they carry a water, pressure or copied
  route obligation;
- the building deals no direct damage.

### Upgrade direction

- Level 1: one selected exchange and small area;
- Level 2: greater pressure storage and stronger slow;
- Level 3: may briefly run mixed covenant after either other exchange completes.

### Gameplay identity

Mid-game slowing and enemy-layer weakening that changes according to the route
problem rather than enemy species.

## 11. Building III — Garden of Many Debts

Hero handle: `tara_many_debts_garden`  
Tier: late / expensive  
Placement: large restored cell  
Limit: 1

### Appearance concept

A monumental low garden divided into several owner-retaining seed beds. Stone
water dividers, ceramic climate covers and small coloured ownership tabs remain
visibly separate. It is cultivated infrastructure, not a magical forest.

### Ability — Recovery Through Difference

The Garden receives contributions from nearby different building families.
Each distinct family may provide one type of debt:

- Living Timber;
- Memory Stone;
- Zlato of Accord;
- water or route service;
- witness or owner service.

Repeated buildings of the same family count once.

At the end of each wave, the Garden spends its collected mixture on one
player-selected result.

#### Repair harvest

Repairs nearby damaged buildings and reduces recovery time after interruption.

#### Material harvest

Returns a balanced amount of Timber and Stone. It is less efficient than a
specialised worksite but produces both.

#### Holding harvest

For the beginning of the next wave, nearby buildings resist capture and forced
movement. Enemies entering the garden's Road area are slowed by root and water
pressure, not attacked.

### Diversity rule

- two different contributing families unlock the basic harvest;
- three improve its strength;
- four or more unlock the full selected result;
- Grey-captured contributors provide no valid debt;
- identical copies never increase the harvest.

### Cost

The Garden consumes small ongoing contributions. A player who demands maximum
harvest every wave may starve ordinary construction.

### Upgrade direction

- Level 1: accepts three contribution types;
- Level 2: accepts four and improves repair/material output;
- Level 3: accepts all five and unlocks full Holding harvest.

### Gameplay identity

Late economic and defensive amplifier rewarding mixed construction and careful
resource commitments.

## 12. Tara kit relationship

| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| Ground Is Never Empty | faster regrowth and temporary stability on restored cells |
| Returned-Ground Frame | chooses how a damaged cell returns to use |
| Three Covenant Vessels | slows, exposes tunnelling and loosens copied layers |
| Garden of Many Debts | converts diverse local contributions into repair, resources or holding strength |

### Strengths

- strongest long-term terrain recovery;
- flexible slowing against heavy, tunnelling and copied-route enemies;
- improves mixed economy;
- repairs and strengthens clustered buildings;
- converts damaged areas into valuable future positions.

### Weaknesses

- slow initial payoff;
- Returned-Ground Frames temporarily occupy valuable cells;
- no direct attack from hero buildings;
- Covenant mode must match the current threat;
- Garden consumes resources and needs several different building families;
- weak on a map with no damaged or exhausted cells.

### Explicit exclusions

- no magical instant forest growth;
- no poison, thorns, insects or nature projectiles;
- no generic healing aura;
- no creation of resources without material debt;
- no ownership bonus based on ancestry;
- no pristine-land or purity mechanic;
- no single sacred seed replacing distinct community ownership.
