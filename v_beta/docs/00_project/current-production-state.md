## Canon state

- Velimudr's revised gameplay kit is approved. Right of Revision remains his
  passive. His canonical buildings are now Table of Three Interpretations,
  Board of Loud Reading and Circle of Silent Witnesses. They respectively buff
  attacking towers, accumulate an area attack and turn trees plus finite stone
  layers into battlefield control. Their ability and animation direction is
  approved. His canonical ordinary attack is Spoken Word: long range, light
  damage and temporary defence reduction on one target layer. He receives his
  Table's selected support mode while inside its radius. Exact numerical balance
  and frame counts remain unapproved.
- Tara's revised gameplay kit is approved. Ground Is Never Empty remains her
  passive. Returned-Ground Frame now controls a Road through living soil,
  recoverable pressure-stone AoE or flood displacement. Three Covenant Vessels
  buff attacking towers through water contracts. Garden of Many Debts chooses
  between an accumulated area attack and post-wave repair. Ability and animation
  direction is approved. Her canonical ordinary attack is Perun's Arrow: high
  damage at short-medium range with one weaker chain, whose path is improved by
  water without gaining extra jumps. Tara receives the selected Vessel contract
  while inside its radius. Exact numerical balance and frame counts remain
  unapproved. Her divine kinship remains a later legend.
- Velimudr and Tara's character identities and static appearances are approved.
  Velimudr is the later-named Belovodye keeper of attributed speech; Tara is the
  first titled Keeper of Returned Ground.
- The previously approved Grey Instructor and subsequent Grey Vessel drafts
  were rejected on 2026-07-27 because they preceded a clear combat-role
  hierarchy. Enemy redesign now starts from ground, air and invisible movement
  classes, each with runner, heavy, roadside melee and extended-range attacker
  roles. The twelve standard types remain approved. Eight ground/air bosses are
  active; all four invisible bosses are deferred and excluded from current
  production. Individual lore, appearance, abilities and animations proceed one
  standard type at a time.
- Ground runner slot 1/12 is approved: Lizard Sprinter follows the Road, attacks
  no structures and gains one interruptible run-up. Its boss, Messenger of Seven
  Burrows, automatically performs one long Road-bound sprint after every seven
  steps. Slow shortens the sprint; no separate target, mechanism or meter exists.
- Ground heavy slot 2/12 is approved in simplified form: Stoneback carries three
  sequential breakable slabs. Its boss, Barrow Colossus, automatically loses
  one of three slabs at 75%, 50% and 25% health and becomes easier to slow and
  push. All towers always target the boss; no slab target or extra meter exists.
- Ground melee slot 3/12 is approved: Grey Breaker leaves the Road only for a
  first-row structure and strikes it with one two-jaw clamp. Its boss, Senior
  Closer, automatically repeats a three-hit series against one first-row target;
  every hit is stronger than the previous one. Knockback breaks contact and
  resets the series, while no hit disables the tower module.
- Ground ranged slot 4/12 is approved: Antlan Slinger remains on the Road and
  attacks first or second-row targets through a visible three-disc cycle. Its
  boss, Fatta Measurer, automatically makes every third shot heavy; that shot
  deals high damage to the selected structure and reduced damage to one adjacent
  cell, then enters a long reload. No manual projectile choice is used.
- Global enemy cleanup is approved: after a short defeat animation, bodies,
  weapons, armour, discs, plates, shadows, markers and debris are removed. Enemy
  death never leaves persistent obstacles or decorative clutter unless a
  mission-specific exception is separately approved.
- Each hero has detection through one existing building rather than a fourth
  structure: Spas uses Warning Stone, Velimudr uses Circle of Silent Witnesses,
  and Tara uses Returned-Ground Frame in flood mode. Detection is local and
  reveals invisible enemies briefly; it does not deal automatic extra damage.
- Air runner slot 5/12 is approved: Vaitmana Courier follows an air route,
  attacks no structures and gains one interruptible folded-wing acceleration.
  Its boss, Courier of Three Skies, automatically rises and gains speed at 66%
  and 33% health while remaining targetable by all anti-air towers. It has no
  separate height meter, interruptible route event or invulnerability.
- Air heavy slot 6/12 is approved: Air Freighter is a slow durable Vaitmana with
  one inertial buffer against ordinary slow. Its boss, Fatta Sky Anchor,
  automatically loses one ballast block at 75%, 50% and 25% health, descends and
  becomes easier to control. Only anti-air can target it at every height; blocks
  have no separate health or meter and disappear before reaching the Road.
- Air melee slot 7/12 is approved: Winged Peck makes one dive against a first-row
  structure and remains targetable only by anti-air. Its boss, Serpent of Three
  Runs, performs an interruptible three-dive series; the final dive deals high
  structure damage and pushes an adjacent hero one valid cell away.
- Air ranged slot 8/12 is approved: Grey Needler attacks first or second-row
  structures and slows the next physical projectile. Its boss, Sky Rangefinder,
  fires an interruptible three-needle sequence; with one target, all three hit
  it with successively reduced damage. Only anti-air can target the type.
- Invisible runner slot 9/12 is approved without a boss: Nav Trace follows the
  Road, attacks no structures and becomes permanently visible after its first
  successful local detection. Its deferred boss concept is non-canonical.
- Invisible heavy slot 10/12 is approved without a boss: Nav Frame is slow and
  durable, leaves strong pressure traces, resists slow and knockback, and becomes
  permanently visible after its first successful local detection.
- Invisible melee slot 11/12 is approved without a boss: Nav Grip attacks only
  first-row structures, gradually reveals its silhouette before the first hit,
  becomes targetable at full reveal and never hides again.
- Invisible ranged slot 12/12 is approved without a boss: Nav Tensioner attacks
  first or second-row structures, becomes targetable during its visible aim,
  loses the prepared shot to a strong hit and never hides after full reveal.
- The standard enemy roster is now complete: twelve roles across ground, air

## Visual and technical locks

- built-in ImageGen 2 for future artistic raster generation;
- exact top-down orthographic 90° gameplay objects;
- premium handmade board-game construction;
- smooth solid punchboard and printed paper, never corrugated packaging;
- every physical part has its own die-cut perimeter, edge, gap and local shadow;
- moderately cartooned proportions;
- lore action precedes visual mechanism;
- no inherited weapons or genre-class abilities;
- Circle combinations join verbs rather than weapon upgrade trees;
- one object family per atlas;
- runtime atlas no larger than 5 MB;
- manifest JSON owns rects, pivots, attachments, drawOrder and animations;
- review grid and composite remain separate human-QA files.
